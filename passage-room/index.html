The Passage Room

Annotated passages from classic literature.

Thirty passages from drama, fiction, poetry, autobiography, essay and epic, with numbered notes on language, structure, context and interpretation attached to the original words.

Method · 01

Identify repeated words, sentence structure, imagery and other specific textual evidence.

Method · 02

Add historical or literary context where it affects the interpretation.

Method · 03

Explain competing interpretations when the evidence supports more than one reading.

Each page reproduces a short passage in wording checked against a public-domain source, and explains selected words, sentence structures, images and formal choices. The notes distinguish textual evidence from interpretation, and say so when a reading is one possible reading.

Use a passage before or after reading the full chapter or scene, for independent study, or as the basis of a lesson: most pages include a before-reading exercise, a classroom task and a bridge into essay work.

About the collection

How the passages are selected.

The collection covers drama, novels, poetry, autobiography, the essay, detective fiction and epic, from Homer to 1929. Passages are chosen where close attention genuinely pays: a word whose meaning has shifted, a sentence whose shape is its argument, an image the rest of the work answers. Wording is checked against public-domain texts, and each page names the sources it consulted.
