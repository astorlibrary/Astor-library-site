Annotated passages from classic literature.

Fifteen passages from drama, fiction, poetry, autobiography and epic, with line-by-line notes on language, structure, context and interpretation.

Each page reproduces a short passage and explains selected words, sentence structures, images and formal choices. The notes distinguish textual evidence from interpretation.

Use a passage before or after reading the full chapter or scene, for independent study, or as the basis of a lesson and discussion.
