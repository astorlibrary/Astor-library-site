Romantic & Regency

Lyrical Ballads

Thirty-six poems from the 1798 and 1800 editions preserve the forms in which Wordsworth and Coleridge first published them, including the original Rime of the Ancyent Marinere.Open book page →

Romantic & Regency

Selected Poems of Samuel Taylor Coleridge

Forty-three poems with line numbering, explanatory footnotes, historical context and critical perspectives. The selection follows Arthur Symons’s 1905 Poems of Coleridge and the text established by James Dykes Campbell, with the Ancient Mariner’s marginal gloss restored beside the verse.Open book page →

Romantic & Regency

The Rime of the Ancient Mariner

Coleridge’s mariner tells of an albatross killed without explanation, a motionless sea, supernatural punishment and a compulsion to repeat a story whose redemption never becomes simple.Open book page →
