Three Men in a Boat

J., George and Harris decide that what they need is a rest, and take a boat up the Thames from Kingston to Oxford with a fox terrier called Montmorency. Jerome K. Jerome meant the book to be a guide to the river with jokes in it; the jokes took over. This edition reproduces A. Frederics’s illustrations of 1889.Open book page →
