Biography

Jerome’s life.

Jerome Klapka Jerome was born in Walsall in 1859 and grew up in East London, where his father’s business failures kept the family poor. He left school at fourteen to work as a railway clerk at Euston, and tried acting, teaching and journalism before his first books, sketches of stage life and the humorous essays of Idle Thoughts of an Idle Fellow (1886), began to sell.

Three Men in a Boat (1889) was written soon after his marriage, from boating trips with his friends George Wingrave and Carl Hentschel, and began as a guide to the Thames with comic interludes. It sold in enormous numbers. Jerome edited The Idler with Robert Barr, wrote plays, drove an ambulance for the French army in the First World War, and died in 1927.
