American Classics

The Open Boat and Other Stories

In January 1897 Stephen Crane spent thirty hours in a dinghy off the Florida coast after the Commodore sank, and ‘The Open Boat’ is the story he made of it. The London edition of 1898 collected it with sixteen other stories in two parts, and Astor follows that order.Open book page →
