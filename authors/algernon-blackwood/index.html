Modern Classics

The Wendigo

A moose-hunting party goes into the backwoods north of Rat Portage, in Canada, and the guide, Défago, is called away in the night by something that shouts his name. Algernon Blackwood published the story in 1910. Astor’s text is set from the Project Gutenberg edition and has not been checked against the 1910 printing.Open book page →

Modern Classics

The Willows

Two men canoeing down the Danube in flood tie up for the night on a sandy island among the willows below Pressburg, and cannot leave. Algernon Blackwood had made the same journey by canoe himself, a few years earlier. This is the original British text of 1907, printed without footnotes.Open book page →
