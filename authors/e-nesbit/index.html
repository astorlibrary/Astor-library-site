Biography

Nesbit’s life.

Edith Nesbit was born in London in 1858; her father, an agricultural chemist, died when she was three. In 1880 she married Hubert Bland, and in 1884 they were among the founders of the Fabian Society. Bland earned little, and Nesbit kept the household, which came to include his two children by another woman, with journalism, verse and stories.

Her books for children, beginning with The Story of the Treasure Seekers (1899), changed the form: her children are funny, quarrelsome and real. The Railway Children ran as a serial in 1905 and came out as a book in 1906. She also wrote ghost and horror stories for adults, collected in Grim Tales (1893) and Fear (1910). She died in Kent in 1924.
