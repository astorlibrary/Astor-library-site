Books and collections.

3 books and collections represented. Each book page identifies its contents, available formats and purchase links. Anthologies include work by more than one writer.

Russian

Notes from Underground

“I am a sick man.... I am a spiteful man.” A retired official, writing notes he says nobody will read, argues that people will always choose freedom over their own good, and then tells how at twenty-four he humiliated a young woman called Liza. Dostoevsky’s short novel of 1864, in Constance Garnett’s translation.Open book page →

Russian

The Brothers Karamazov

First published serially between 1879 and 1880, The Brothers Karamazov centres on the murder of Fyodor Pavlovitch Karamazov and the lives of his sons Dmitri, Ivan and Alyosha.Open book page →

Russian

White Nights

Over four nights on a Petersburg canal embankment, a lonely young dreamer falls in love with Nastenka, who is waiting for another man. Dostoevsky published this short sentimental novel in 1848, four months before his arrest, and revised it in 1860; Constance Garnett’s translation follows the revised text.Open book page →
