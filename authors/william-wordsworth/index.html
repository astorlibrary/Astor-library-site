Biography

Wordsworth’s life.

William Wordsworth was born in 1770 at Cockermouth on the edge of the Lake District and lost both parents before he was fourteen. After Cambridge he spent a year in revolutionary France, where he fell in love with Annette Vallon, who bore him a daughter. Back in England he lived with his sister Dorothy, and in 1797 they moved to Alfoxden in Somerset to be near Coleridge. Lyrical Ballads (1798) came out of that friendship, and Wordsworth added his Preface to the second edition of 1800.

From 1799 he lived in Grasmere, at Dove Cottage and later at Rydal Mount nearby, and in 1802 he married Mary Hutchinson. He became Poet Laureate in 1843 and died in 1850. The Prelude, the long poem about his own growth that he revised for half a century, was published a few months after his death.
