Russian

The Overcoat

Akaki Akakievitch, a poor copying clerk in St Petersburg, goes without supper for months to pay for a new overcoat, wears it for one day and has it stolen on the way home. Gogol’s story of 1842, in Claud Field’s translation, started a whole line of Russian fiction about poor clerks.Open book page →
