Biography

Le Fanu’s life.

Joseph Sheridan Le Fanu was born in Dublin in 1814 into a family of Huguenot descent; the playwright Richard Brinsley Sheridan was his great-uncle. He studied law at Trinity College, Dublin, and was called to the bar, but went into journalism instead, and from 1861 he owned and edited the Dublin University Magazine. After his wife died in 1858 he withdrew almost entirely from society.

His best-known novel, Uncle Silas, appeared in 1864. Carmilla was serialised in the magazine The Dark Blue in 1871 and 1872 and collected in 1872 in In a Glass Darkly, framed, like the other stories there, as a case from the papers of the physician Dr Hesselius. Le Fanu died in Dublin in 1873.
