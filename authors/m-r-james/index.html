Biography

M. R. James’s life.

Montague Rhodes James was born in Kent in 1862 and grew up at Great Livermere in Suffolk, where his father was rector. He spent almost his whole life at Eton and at King’s College, Cambridge: he was a student, then a fellow, Director of the Fitzwilliam Museum, Provost of King’s and finally Provost of Eton, where he died in 1936. His scholarly work was on medieval manuscripts, and he catalogued the manuscripts of nearly every Cambridge college.

The ghost stories began as entertainments read aloud to friends at Christmas. Ghost Stories of an Antiquary appeared in 1904, three more volumes followed, and the collected stories were published in 1931. Many of his ghosts come out of the things he knew best: old books, church furnishings and college archives.
