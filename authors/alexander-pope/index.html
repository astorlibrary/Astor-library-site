Restoration & Enlightenment

The Rape of the Lock and An Essay on Criticism

A young lord snips a curl from a beauty’s head at Hampton Court, and Alexander Pope tells the story as an epic, with sylphs for guardian spirits, a card game for a battle and a new star at the end. This edition pairs The Rape of the Lock with An Essay on Criticism, the verse argument about judging poetry that made Pope’s name in 1711, and prints the two poems in their later forms.Open book page →
