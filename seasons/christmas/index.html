Your Christmas reading list

The Christmas collection brings together the dedicated seasonal books in the Astor catalogue: four individual Dickens Christmas books, their collected edition, American Christmas Stories and the cultural history A Victorian Christmas.

The shelves also follow two older forms of Christmas entertainment: telling ghost stories and staging festive comedy. Free guides on Dickens, Christmas traditions and supernatural fiction sit alongside close readings and the A Christmas Carol study edition.

The Astor reading note

Christmas traditions have histories

A Victorian Christmas places familiar objects and customs within changes in postage, transport, publishing, commerce and household work. It also looks back to practices established before Victoria’s reign. Its account allows a tree, a card, a carol or a Christmas dinner to be studied as a particular historical subject.

The literary collections preserve a similar distinction between a story and its context. Dickens at Christmas contains four of the five short Christmas books Dickens published between 1843 and 1848. American Christmas Stories contains eight stories and a poem dated 1823–1905, supported by publication histories and historical chapters. The two collections have different contents and should be chosen accordingly.
