Your Summer reading list

Summer reading here begins with places and activities present in the books: a Thames boating holiday, the riverbank in The Wind in the Willows, outdoor childhood adventures, lakes and gardens. Several novels show the social world of a summer visit or resort rather than a simple holiday escape.

The Shakespeare shelf gives theatre and reading groups a choice of forest, pastoral and island settings. The pages below bring the editions together with the relevant guides, study material and Passage Room close readings.

The Astor reading note

Choose a place, then compare it

A river sequence can begin with Three Men in a Boat and continue with The Wind in the Willows or Adventures of Huckleberry Finn. Compare the work of travelling, the relation between river and shore, and the narrator’s treatment of the people encountered along the way.

For a shorter group programme, place the opening of Mrs Dalloway beside the summer gathering in The Great Gatsby or the Grand Isle chapters of The Awakening. The seasonal setting can then be studied through specific details of weather, visiting, clothing, leisure and social expectation.
