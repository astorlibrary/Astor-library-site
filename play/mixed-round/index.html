Revision game · one book

Mixed round

Speakers, missing words, themes, techniques, characters and the order of events, mixed together and drawn from one title. Nothing repeats its footing twice in a row.

Why this one

What it is for.

Drilling one kind of question teaches the shape of the question as much as the book. A round that changes its kind every time only rewards knowing the text.

Keyboard: number keys choose an answer, Enter checks and moves on, R restarts at the end.
