Quiz

Ten questions

Lines due for another look come first, then ones you haven’t met yet, with one question on the people and one on the plot.

Why this helps

Drilling one kind of question teaches the shape of the question as much as the book. A quiz that changes the kind of question every time only rewards knowing the text.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 4 or A to D to answer, Enter to go on, and R at the end to start again.
