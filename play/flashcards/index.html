Learn the lines

Flashcards

Ten cards at a time, three ways to learn them. The lines you forget come back sooner; the ones you know move up a box.

  1. Choose Recall, Complete or Memorise
  2. Think of the answer, then turn the card
  3. Mark yourself honestly

How the boxes work

Every key line lives in one of five boxes. A new line starts in the first and comes back the next day; each time you know it, it moves up a box and waits longer: two days, four, eight, sixteen. Miss it and it goes back to the first. A line answered in any quiz moves the same way, so the flashcards and the quizzes always agree.

On a keyboard: Space turns the card, the right arrow says Got it, the left arrow Not yet. On a touch screen, swipe a turned card right or left. Your cards are saved in this browser only.
