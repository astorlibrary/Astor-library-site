Order the plot

Put the acts, chapters and scenes back in order.

Why this helps

Knowing what happens before and after a scene lets you write about cause, not just list events. It is also the fastest way to find a quotation you half remember.

On a keyboard, Tab to a word or an arrow and press Space to use it, then Enter to check your answer and again to go on.
