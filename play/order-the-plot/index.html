Order the plot

Put the acts, chapters and scenes back in order.

  1. Read the shuffled events
  2. Drag them into order, earliest first
  3. Check, and see where each belongs

Why this helps

Knowing what happens before and after a scene lets you write about cause and effect. It is also the fastest way to find a quotation you half remember.

On a keyboard: Tab to a word or an arrow and press Space to use it, then Enter to check and again to go on.
