Revision game · one book

Fill the line

Words are taken out of a speech and mixed into a bank with decoys. Put the right ones back where they belong.

Why this one

What it is for.

A quotation you can only half remember is a quotation you cannot use. Rebuilding a line word by word is the fastest way to learn it exactly, and the decoys are drawn from the same book so nothing is given away by register alone.

Keyboard: number keys choose an answer, Enter checks and moves on, R restarts at the end.
