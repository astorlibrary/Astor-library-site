Who said it?

A line appears without its speaker. Name who says it, and read why it matters to them rather than to anybody else in the book.

Why this one

What it is for.

Attribution is where quotation marks stop being decoration. Knowing that Lady Macbeth says one line and the Doctor the other is the difference between a paragraph about the play and a paragraph about a character.

Keyboard: number keys choose an answer, Enter checks and moves on, R restarts at the end.
