Who said it?

Read a line and name who says it.

Why this helps

If you can’t say who speaks a line, you can’t use it to write about a character. Knowing which lines belong to whom is the difference between a paragraph about the book and a paragraph about a person in it.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 4 or A to D to answer, Enter to go on, and R at the end to start again.
