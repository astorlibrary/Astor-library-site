Guide summary

Romeo and Juliet

The guide retraces the play’s action and characters, ties its themes to the world Shakespeare wrote for, and marks the quotations most worth committing to memory.

Textual evidence

Check the language and form

The play’s evidence is dramatic. Note who is on stage and who overhears, how public brawls answer private vows, and how the Prologue announces the ending before the lovers have met.

Study question

How does Shakespeare make the ending of Romeo and Juliet feel inevitable when the Prologue has already given it away?

An answer would set the Prologue’s promise against particular scenes of choice and accident, cited precisely by act and scene.
