What the guide is for.

A period guide for readers of Pope: the Glorious Revolution to 1745, Pope’s life, the Scriblerus Club, coffee-house London and the heroic couplet. Ten slides.

Read The Rape of the Lock with the guide’s chronology open, and set Pope’s mock-epic beside the London of coffee houses and Grub Street.

Reading notes

Reading the text and its history.

Use these questions alongside the 10 illustrated slides and the work itself.

Context

Place the poem in its year

Find The Rape of the Lock or An Essay on Criticism in the chronology, and note what was happening in politics and in Pope’s life that year.

Circle

Read the Scriblerians together

Use the slide on the Scriblerus Club to connect Pope with Swift and Gay. What targets do their satires share?

Form

Scan a couplet

Take two lines of Pope and mark the rhyme, the pentameter and the pause. How does the couplet sharpen a judgement?

How to use this guide. As a Catholic, Pope was barred from the universities and from public office, and was educated largely at home. Read his early career in the chronology with that in mind.
