Guide summary

Halloween

The guide follows Halloween from Celtic ritual to the present, showing how ghost stories and the great Gothic novels supplied the festival with its imagery of monsters, hauntings and the dark.

Textual evidence

Check the festival’s sources

A festival’s history is read in mixed evidence: the Gothic novels whose monsters Halloween borrowed, the ghost stories told in season, and the songs, periodicals and recollections that record how the night was actually kept.

Study question

How much of the modern Halloween did literature invent?

An answer would pair a custom or image of the festival with its literary source and show when and how the borrowing happened.
