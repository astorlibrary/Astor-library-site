Guide summary

Victorian Christmas

The guide traces how the Victorians assembled the Christmas we still keep, from the tree, the card and the carol revival to the family dinner and the season’s reading.

Textual evidence

Check the dated evidence

A history of customs rests on dated evidence: early Christmas cards, carol collections, the seasonal numbers of the illustrated papers, and Victorians’ own recollections of how the day was kept.

Study question

Which parts of the modern Christmas are genuinely Victorian inventions?

Support the answer with dated evidence such as an early card, a carol book or a periodical’s Christmas number, rather than with later tradition.
