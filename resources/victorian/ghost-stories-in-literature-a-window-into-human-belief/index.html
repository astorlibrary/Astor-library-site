Guide summary

Ghost stories

The guide treats the ghost story as evidence of the living: what a culture’s tales of the dead show about its beliefs, its memories and its fears, from fireside telling to the Victorian ghost story at its height.

Textual evidence

Check the tales and their tellers

The evidence lies in the tales and their tellers. Note who narrates each story, what the teller claims to have seen, and what its first audiences were prepared to believe; then set the literary ghost story beside the folk belief it draws on.

Study question

Do ghost stories depend on their audiences believing in ghosts?

An answer would draw on particular tales, their narrators and their occasions, alongside what is known of the beliefs of those who first told and heard them.
