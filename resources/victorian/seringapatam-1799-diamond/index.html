Reading notes

Reading the text and its history.

Use these questions alongside the 9 illustrated slides and the work itself.

History

Read the prologue as a document

Compare the cousin’s account of the storming with the guide’s dates and names. What does he know at first hand, and what is he guessing?

Objects

Follow the plunder

Set the guide’s account of what left Seringapatam beside Herncastle’s diamond. How does the novel connect one stolen object with an English country house?

Sources

Separate fact and invention

Use the slide on real diamonds to decide which details Collins borrowed and which he invented for the Moonstone.

How to use this guide. The prologue is the only part of the novel set in India. Reread it after the guide, and again when the three Indians reappear in Yorkshire.
