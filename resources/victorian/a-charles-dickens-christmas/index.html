Guide summary

Charles Dickens

The guide follows Dickens’s part in remaking the English Christmas, showing how A Christmas Carol and the Christmas books that followed it bound the season to charity, family and the fireside.

Textual evidence

Check the books and the customs

The evidence here is partly literary and partly social. Test the guide’s claims against the Christmas books themselves, beginning with A Christmas Carol, and against what Victorians recorded of their own festivities in letters, periodicals and the illustrated papers.

Study question

How far was the Victorian Christmas Dickens’s invention?

An answer would set scenes from A Christmas Carol and the later Christmas books against customs the Victorians already kept, citing both.

How to use this guide. Check what the guide says against the text itself, and quote the book, not the slides.
