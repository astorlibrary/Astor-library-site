Guide summary

Wide Sargasso Sea

A close reading of the novel’s long middle section, where the husband takes over the telling. It follows the marriage at Granbois from first unease to open breakdown, and watches narration, rumour and money tilt the balance of power.

Textual evidence

Check the language and form

Most of this section reaches us through the husband’s voice, briefly interrupted by Antoinette’s, and that handover matters. Daniel Cosway’s letters, the talk of obeah and the renaming of Antoinette as Bertha are the crucial pieces of evidence.

Study question

How far is the husband responsible for the collapse of the marriage in Part 2 of Wide Sargasso Sea?

Weigh his narration against what it lets slip: the letters he chooses to believe, the name he imposes and the interlude in which Antoinette speaks.
