What the guide is for.

A period guide for readers of Coleridge and Keats: two generations of poets, the events from the Bastille to the Reform Act, Lyrical Ballads, the reviewers, the places and the forms. Eleven slides.

Read a poem by Coleridge or Keats with the guide’s timeline and list of forms beside you, and place it among the events and quarrels of its decade.

Guide contents

What the guide covers.

The complete guide has 11 slides. It covers:

  1. 01

    The span of the period and its two generations of poets.

  2. 02

    Events in Britain and Europe, from the Bastille to Peterloo and the Reform Act.

  3. 03

    Lyrical Ballads and the key publications from 1789 to 1850.

  4. 04

    The reviewers, the places, the forms the poets used, and terms such as the sublime.

Reading notes

Reading the text and its history.

Generations

Form

Name the form

Identify the form of a poem you are studying from the guide’s list, from the ballad to ottava rima, and ask why the poet chose it.

How to use this guide. Lyrical Ballads was published anonymously in 1798, and a second edition with Wordsworth’s Preface followed in 1800. Check which edition your text follows.
