Guide contents

What the guide covers.

The complete guide has 9 slides. It covers:

  1. 01

    What domestic tragedy is, and the plays from 1592 to 1608.

  2. 02

    The murder of Thomas Arden in 1551 and the 1592 quarto of Arden of Faversham.

  3. 03

    Walter Calverley’s crimes in 1605 and the 1608 quarto of A Yorkshire Tragedy.

  4. 04

    Petty treason, pressing to death and further reading.

Sources

Compare the crime and the play

Read the guide’s account of the Faversham murder beside the play’s plotting scenes. What did the dramatist add to the chronicle?

Attribution

Weigh the title pages

Arden appeared with no author named; A Yorkshire Tragedy was printed as Shakespeare’s. Use the guide to ask what a title page proves.

Law

Read the law into the plot

Apply the terms slide to Alice Arden and to Calverley. How does the law of the period shape what the audience expects to see punished?

How to use this guide. Both plays were sold as true stories. Keep the guide’s record of each crime in view as you read, and notice where each play departs from it.
