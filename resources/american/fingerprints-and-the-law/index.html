Evidence

Date Wilson’s method

The trial in Pudd’nhead Wilson takes place decades before any court accepted fingerprint evidence. Use the timeline to judge how far ahead of his time Twain places Wilson.

Composition

Find the twins

Read the slide on the Capello twins and Those Extraordinary Twins. What survives in the novel of the comic story Twain began with?

How to use this guide. Galton’s book appeared in 1892, the year before the novel began its serial run in The Century Magazine. Read Wilson’s courtroom demonstration with those dates in mind.
