Guide contents

What the guide covers.

The complete guide has 12 slides. It covers:

  1. 01

    Serialisation in the Pictorial Review, the book of October 1920 and the 1921 Pulitzer Prize.

  2. 02

    The setting, the story in outline and the characters.

  3. 03

    Old New York’s places and terms, families and customs.

  4. 04

    The title, a chronology of Wharton’s life and the adaptations.

Reading notes

Reading the text and its history.

Setting

Read the opening night

The novel opens at a performance of Faust at the Academy of Music. Use the guide to identify who is in the boxes and what their places mean.

Characters

Follow Archer’s choice

Use the character slide to follow Newland Archer between May Welland and Ellen Olenska. What does each woman stand for in his mind?

Distance

Read across fifty years

Wharton wrote in 1920 about the 1870s. Use the chronology to ask how that distance shapes the narrator’s judgements.

How to use this guide. The novel was serialised in the Pictorial Review, a New York women’s magazine, before it appeared as a book in October 1920.
