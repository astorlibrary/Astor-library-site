A period guide for Moby-Dick: the fleet at its height, the ports and the whales, the lay, the Essex, Melville’s own voyage and the industry’s decline. Twelve slides.

Guide contents

What the guide covers.

The complete guide has 12 slides. It covers:

  1. 01

    The fleet at its height, the ports of New Bedford and Nantucket, and the whales hunted.

  2. 02

    Whale oil, spermaceti and the long Pacific voyages.

  3. 03

    The lay, the sinking of the Essex in 1820 and Melville’s voyage on the Acushnet.

  4. 04

    Terms from the trade, the decline after 1859 and further reading.
