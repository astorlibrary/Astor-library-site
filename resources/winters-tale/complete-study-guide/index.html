Reading notes

What sixteen years can—and cannot—repair

The play changes country, generation and dramatic form, but it does not pretend that time simply erases what Leontes has done.

Sicilia

Jealousy becomes government

Leontes converts a private certainty into public law. Because the accusation comes from a king, misreading damages a marriage, a court, a child and the future of the state.

Bohemia

Another kind of play begins

The pastoral world makes room for youth, music, disguise and comic energy. Yet class and paternal authority remain active, so the change of tone is an experiment rather than an escape.

The statue

Wonder does not cancel loss

The final scene offers restoration while keeping its mechanism uncertain. Hermione can return; Mamillius cannot. Reconciliation matters because the play preserves that difference.

How to use this guide. When the play changes tone, do not leave the earlier acts behind. Ask what Bohemia remembers from Sicilia and what the final wonder is unable to restore.
