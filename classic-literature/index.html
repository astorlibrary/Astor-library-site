Eight literature collections.

Ancient & Epic

The Odyssey asks what remains of a man after war and wandering. The Aeneid turns the refugee from Troy towards a future Rome, never forgetting what that future costs.

Homer and Virgil →

Renaissance & Early Modern

Thomas More imagines an island society whose customs unsettle European assumptions. Machiavelli studies power as it is gained, kept and lost.

More and Machiavelli →

Shakespeare

All thirty-seven plays and two narrative poems, with sources, first printings, explanatory notes, performance history and study material.

Forty-one editions →

Restoration & Enlightenment

Private letters and improbable voyages become ways of testing authority. Richardson follows a servant’s struggle for safety; Swift sends Gulliver into societies that expose his own.

Richardson and Swift →

Romantic & Regency

Austen makes courtship answer to money, inheritance and self-knowledge. Mary Shelley gives scientific ambition a voice, a body and the power to accuse its creator.

Austen and Shelley →

Victorian Literature

Dickens, Brontë, Hardy, Stevenson, Stoker, Wilde, Wells and Conan Doyle move between city streets, country houses, moors, laboratories and consulting rooms.

Eleven books →

American Classics

Douglass, Stowe, Melville, Twain, Crane and London write about freedom in a nation repeatedly denying it: on plantations, rivers, battlefields, ships and frozen trails.

Six books →

Modern Classics

Chesterton turns political conspiracy into a strange chase through London. Orwell follows a revolution whose language changes as thoroughly as its promises.

Edition contents

What Astor editions contain.

Book pages provide plot and structural summaries, publication history, historical context, analysis of selected passages and information about major adaptations. They are designed for use alongside the complete work.

Published prose editions include complete texts, chapter summaries and factual context. Shakespeare editions include complete plays, scene summaries, line numbers and explanatory notes. Study editions add quotations, critical interpretations, model paragraphs and essay support.
