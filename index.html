Inside the books

What an Astor page looks like

Pages from four editions. A main edition has the complete work with summaries, notes and line numbers on the page. A study edition adds quotations with analysis, the critics and model paragraphs.

MacbethThe complete play, scene summaries, line numbers and footnotes on the same page as the text. Othello, study editionKey quotations with analysis, and the critics, beside the text. The Rime of the Ancient MarinerThe poem with Coleridge’s marginal gloss, line numbers and footnotes. The OdysseyButler’s prose translation, a summary before each book and short notes on the background.
