Account and resource privacy.

This notice covers the free accounts used to continue through complete Astor Library educational resources, the private resource shelf and the separate choice to receive occasional marketing emails.

Information collected.

When you create an account, Astor Library records your email address, an account identifier, account timestamps and whether you selected the optional marketing-email box. If you use the personal resource shelf, it also stores the identifier of a saved or opened resource, when it was saved or last opened and the last slide reached. It does not copy the resource itself into your account or keep a separate event for every slide view. Supabase Auth processes the password and stores a salted password hash; Astor Library does not store or display your password.

Why it is used.

Your email address and authentication session are used to create and secure the account, send confirmation or password-recovery messages, and allow access beyond the public resource preview. Saved and recently opened resource references are used only to provide your private resource shelf and continue links. Marketing email is a separate purpose and relies on your positive consent.

Marketing consent.

The marketing box is not pre-selected and is not required for resource access. If you select it, Astor Library records the current choice, when consent was given, the place where it was recorded and the wording version. You can withdraw consent on the account page or by emailing support@astorlibrary.com. Withdrawal does not remove access to resources.

Services used.

Supabase provides account authentication and the private account database. Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website, handles authenticated resource requests and provides the Turnstile security check used to limit automated account abuse. Their processing and any international transfers are governed by the settings and agreements attached to the site owner’s accounts with those services.

Cookies.

Account access uses strictly necessary, secure session cookies so that you remain signed in and protected slides can be served. These cookies are not used for advertising. Signing out removes the active Astor Library session cookies from the browser.

Private resource shelf.

Saved resources and recent activity are visible only to the signed-in account that created them. Recent activity is deduplicated so that each resource has one current last-viewed record. The account page shows the latest 20 recently opened resources, and unsaved activity is pruned beyond the latest 50 resources. Saved rows remain until you remove them or delete the account. You can remove a saved resource at any time without affecting resource access.

Retention and deletion.

The active account record, saved-resource references and deduplicated recent activity are kept while the account is in use. The account page provides a self-service deletion control; deleting the account removes those active records. Provider backups may be retained for the limited periods set in the site owner’s configured service agreements. If a marketing provider is connected later, any minimal suppression record needed to honour an unsubscribe must be handled and disclosed separately.

Access and correction.

You can see the account email, request a confirmed change to the sign-in email, review the current marketing choice, saved resources and recent activity on the account page. You can unsubscribe from marketing without deleting the account, remove saved resources individually or delete the account separately. For another correction or data-protection request, email support@astorlibrary.com. UK residents can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Public pages.

Books, publishing information, About pages, subject pages and other ordinary site content do not require an account. Resource landing pages and the first three presentation slides are also public.
