Study editions/Henry IV, Part 2

William Shakespeare

Henry IV, Part 2

The sequel begins with a lie: Rumour tells Northumberland that his son won at Shrewsbury, and the truth arrives a scene later. Everything after that runs on delay and bad news. The king cannot sleep, Falstaff cannot pay, the rebels are tricked out of their army with a promise, and two old justices in Gloucestershire count their dead friends and the price of bullocks at Stamford fair. Hal takes the crown from his father's pillow before the man is dead, and in the last scene the new king walks past the old friend who has ridden all night to meet him. It is the play where Part 1's jokes come back with the cost attached.
