Study editions/Henry IV, Part 2

William Shakespeare

Henry IV, Part 2

The rebellion Hotspur led has failed at Shrewsbury, but the war goes on. Northumberland, Hotspur's father, raises a second rising with the Archbishop of York and then withdraws to Scotland, and Prince John ends it at Gaultree Forest by promising the rebels redress and arresting them once their army has gone home. King Henry, sleepless and ill, fears what his son will be as king. Prince Hal keeps company with Falstaff less and less; Falstaff, older and poorer, recruits soldiers in Gloucestershire, borrows a thousand pounds from an old acquaintance, Justice Shallow, and rides to London when the King dies, expecting to be made great. The new King Henry V keeps the Lord Chief Justice who once sent him to prison, and in the street after his coronation tells Falstaff he does not know him.

1600A quarto whose first copies left out the whole of 3.1; the scene was added in a reissue the same year.
Rumour and a dancerRumour, painted full of tongues, opens the play with false news of Shrewsbury; a dancer speaks the epilogue and promises more of Falstaff.
I know thee not, old manThe new king's first public words to Falstaff, in the street after the coronation.
