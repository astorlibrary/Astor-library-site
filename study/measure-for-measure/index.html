Study editions/Measure for Measure

William Shakespeare

Measure for Measure

A duke who has let the sexual laws lapse for years hands Vienna to a deputy known for severity, then stays behind disguised as a friar to watch what happens. Angelo condemns a man for getting his betrothed with child, and then offers the man's sister his life in exchange for her body. The comedy machinery — a substitution in bed, a substituted head, a public hearing — arrives on time and saves everyone it is supposed to save, and the play still ends with a marriage proposal that nobody on stage answers.
