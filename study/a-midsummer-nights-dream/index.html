Study editions/A Midsummer Night's Dream

William Shakespeare

A Midsummer Night's Dream

A father asks the Duke of Athens to have his daughter killed if she will not marry the man he has chosen, and the comedy proceeds from there. Four young people run into a wood where a quarrelling fairy king and queen are already using a drug that makes the sleeper love whatever they see first, and a company of Athenian tradesmen are rehearsing a tragedy they cannot act. The play's interest is that magic does not invent the lovers' cruelty to one another; it only speeds it up.
