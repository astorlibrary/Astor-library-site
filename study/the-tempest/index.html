Study editions/The Tempest

William Shakespeare

The Tempest

A magician who was once Duke of Milan raises a storm to bring his enemies ashore on the island where he has lived in exile for twelve years. He has the whole afternoon planned before the play begins, and the interest lies in watching a man with absolute power discover what he actually wants to do with it. Almost alone among Shakespeare's plays it keeps to a single place and a few hours, so that the action and the performance take roughly the same time.

c. 1610-11After reports of the 1609 Sea Venture wreck reached London, and before the recorded court performance of November 1611.
1623No quarto exists. The First Folio prints it first in the volume, at the head of the comedies.
No single narrative sourceShipwreck pamphlets, Florio's Montaigne, Ovid and the court masque, combined rather than adapted.
Five acts, nine scenesAct I Scene 2 alone runs to about a third of the play.

Where it happens

  • Milan The dukedom Prospero lost to his brother while he studied.
  • Naples Alonso's kingdom, and the wedding party the shipwrecked court was returning from.
  • Tunis Where Claribel was married, and the voyage home from which the storm interrupts.
  • Algiers Argier in the text: the city from which Sycorax was banished to the island.
  • Bermuda The still-vex'd Bermoothes of Ariel's speech, and the site of the 1609 wreck behind the play's storm.

Where the quotations come from.

Every quotation on this page was located in Globe/Moby text of The Tempest before it was published, and carries its act.scene.line, Globe/Moby line numbering. Line numbers follow the traditional Globe numbering; modern editions differ by a line or two. Ariel's songs are printed inside another speaker's text in some settings, and are attributed here to Ariel.
