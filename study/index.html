Study Editions

The Astor Study Editions are printed books for students at school and university. Each prints the text with closer analysis than a standard Astor edition: commentary on the scenes or chapters, key quotations, characters and themes, and, in many editions, critical perspectives and help with essays and exam answers.

Every standard Astor edition already has a summary of each chapter or scene and explanatory notes. What each Study Edition adds is listed on its page. The Study Editions are separate from the free study guides on this site.
