Astor Study

Study editions.

Every study edition carries the complete text, summaries of each scene or chapter, the quotations that matter with analysis, the critical arguments, and model paragraphs you can take apart. No exam board sets the contents.

Main Astor editions contain the complete texts. Their supporting material includes chapter or scene summaries, explanatory notes and context.

Single-text editions cover one work. Paired editions contain study material for two commonly studied texts in one volume.
