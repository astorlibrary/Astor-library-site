Study editions/The Comedy of Errors

William Shakespeare

The Comedy of Errors

Two sets of identical twins, separated as infants in a shipwreck, end up in the same city on the same day without knowing it. For five acts the wrong master beats the wrong servant, the wrong husband is locked out of his own dinner, and a gold chain, a purse and a length of rope travel between people who each believe the other has gone mad. Shakespeare doubles Plautus’s one pair of twins into two, which quadruples the mistakes, and then wraps the whole machine in a story of an old man under sentence of death who has spent five years looking for his family. The farce is timed to the hour; the frame is timed to a lifetime.
