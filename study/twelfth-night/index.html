William Shakespeare

Twelfth Night

A girl washed up in a country she has never heard of puts on her drowned brother's kind of clothes and goes to work for a duke. Everything that follows comes from that one decision: a messenger who is loved by the wrong person, a household that turns a joke into an imprisonment, and a recognition scene that has to give two people back a twin before anybody can marry.
