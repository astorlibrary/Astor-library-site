Study editions/Great Expectations

Charles Dickens

Great Expectations

A blacksmith's boy who once fed a starving convict on the marshes is given a fortune by an anonymous benefactor and sent to London to be made into a gentleman. He assumes the money comes from the woman in the decayed wedding dress, and it does not. The book is narrated by the older Pip, who knows from the first page exactly how badly the younger one behaved to the people who loved him, and declines to spare either of them.
