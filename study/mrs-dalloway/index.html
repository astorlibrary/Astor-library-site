Study editions/Mrs Dalloway

Virginia Woolf

Mrs Dalloway

A woman of fifty-two buys flowers, mends a dress and gives a party; a shell-shocked veteran sits in Regent's Park hearing the dead speak and, at six o'clock, throws himself out of a window. The two never meet, and Woolf joins them with clocks, traffic and a phrase from Cymbeline, so that the news of a stranger's death reaches Clarissa in the middle of her own success and she recognises it. The whole book takes one day.

Where the quotations come from.

Every quotation on this page was located in Standard Ebooks text of Mrs Dalloway before it was published, and carries its episode in the day — the novel has no chapters or numbered sections, so quotations are placed by the moment they fall in. The source consulted runs continuously, without chapter headings or numbered divisions, so references here name the episode rather than a chapter. Printed editions mark Woolf's white-space breaks but do not number them.
