William Shakespeare

The Taming of the Shrew

A tinker is picked up drunk outside an alehouse and persuaded by a bored lord that he is a nobleman recovering from madness. The play performed for him is about a man who marries a woman for her dowry and then starves and contradicts her into agreement, and about a younger sister whose suitors buy their way into her house disguised as tutors. Shakespeare never returns to the tinker, so the audience is left holding a frame with nothing on the other end of it.
