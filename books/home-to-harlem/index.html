Home to Harlem

McKay’s complete 1928 novel is presented in its original three parts and twenty-one chapters, with summaries, contextual notes and explanatory footnotes on its Harlem world.

Astor annotated edition

About this edition

The edition places the novel beside McKay’s life, the Harlem Renaissance, the Great Migration, Haiti and the racial violence of 1919.

Supporting material identifies places and references, traces publication and reception, and includes a glossary and bibliography.
