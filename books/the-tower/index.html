Thoor Ballylee

Macmillan published The Tower in London in 1928. It was Yeats’s first major collection since the Nobel Prize of 1923, and its twenty-one poems include “Sailing to Byzantium”, “Leda and the Swan”, “Among School Children”, “Meditations in Time of Civil War” and “Nineteen Hundred and Nineteen”.

The tower of the title is Thoor Ballylee, the Norman tower in County Galway that Yeats bought in 1917 and restored. Thomas Sturge Moore’s design for the cover, stamped in gold on green cloth, shows the tower reflected in the water below it.
