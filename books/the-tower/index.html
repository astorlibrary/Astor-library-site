W. B. Yeats

The Tower

Yeats’s twenty-one poems appear in the original 1928 order, with his first-edition notes, numbered lines and explanatory footnotes. Every poem has its own summary and historical context.

Astor annotated edition
Paperback · Edited by Haydn Wood

View paperback edition

What this Astor edition contains

  • All twenty-one poems in the 1928 order
  • Numbered lines and explanatory footnotes
  • Individual poem summaries and historical context
  • Yeats’s original notes, glossary and further reading

About this edition

The supporting material places the poems beside Thoor Ballylee, the Irish War of Independence and Civil War, Yeats’s work in the Senate, Irish folklore and A Vision.

The edition includes critical perspectives, a glossary and further reading while preserving the collection’s original order and Yeats’s own notes.
