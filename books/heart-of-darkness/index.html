Heart of Darkness

First published in three instalments in Blackwood’s Edinburgh Magazine from February to April 1899, Heart of Darkness appeared in book form in Youth: A Narrative, and Two Other Stories on 13 November 1902.

Text and supporting material

  • The complete 1902 book text
  • Explanatory footnotes throughout
  • Historical context on the Congo Free State
  • Critical perspectives and a documentary record

Marlow’s journey upriver in search of the ivory agent Kurtz draws heavily on Conrad’s own experience in the Congo Free State in 1890, while transforming the historical record into one of the most debated works of modern English fiction.

This edition adds original material on Conrad’s life and sources, the Congo Free State, narrative structure, language, major critical debates, reception history and the surviving documentary record.
