Herman Melville

Bartleby, the Scrivener

A Wall Street lawyer hires a copyist who gradually refuses the work he is asked to do. Melville’s complete 1853 story appears with explanatory notes and material on the offices, institutions and working life behind it.

Astor annotated edition
Paperback · Edited by Haydn Wood

View paperback edition

What this Astor edition contains

  • Complete 1853 story
  • Ten editorial reading sections with summaries
  • Explanatory footnotes and historical context
  • Critical perspectives and material on legal work

About this edition

The story is arranged in ten editorial reading sections without changing the narrative. Summaries and historical context accompany the text, with explanatory footnotes and critical perspectives.

The supporting material covers legal copying, Wall Street, the Court of Chancery, the Tombs and the Dead Letter Office.
