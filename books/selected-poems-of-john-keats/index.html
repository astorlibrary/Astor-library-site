Selected Poems of John Keats

All thirteen poems from Keats’s 1820 Lamia, Isabella, The Eve of St. Agnes, and Other Poems appear in their original sequence, with numbered lines, explanatory footnotes and historical context.

About this edition

The selection preserves the shape of the 1820 volume and includes the publishers’ original advertisement. Notes and contextual material sit alongside the poems.

Critical perspectives, manuscripts, reviews, textual variants and later influence are covered in the supporting material, with a glossary for unfamiliar terms.
