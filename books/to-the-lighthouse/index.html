Talland House and Skye

The Hogarth Press, run by Virginia and Leonard Woolf, published To the Lighthouse on 5 May 1927 in a first printing of 3,000 copies; Harcourt Brace published it in New York the same year. It won the Prix Femina–Vie Heureuse in 1928.

The novel is in three parts, “The Window”, “Time Passes” and “The Lighthouse”, and is set at the Ramsays’ summer house on the Isle of Skye. Behind it is Talland House in St Ives, Cornwall, which Woolf’s father, Leslie Stephen, rented for the family’s summers from 1882. Her mother died when Woolf was thirteen. Her sister, Vanessa Bell, told her that reading the book was like seeing their mother “raised from the dead”.
