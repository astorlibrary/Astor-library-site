William Blake

Songs of Innocence and of Experience

First issued separately as Songs of Innocence in 1789 and Songs of Experience in 1794, Blake later brought the two together under the subtitle Shewing the Two Contrary States of the Human Soul. The surviving copies vary in arrangement, colouring and detail because Blake printed and finished them by hand.
