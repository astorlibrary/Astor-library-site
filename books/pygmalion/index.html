George Bernard Shaw

Pygmalion

Shaw’s complete 1916 Constable text follows Eliza Doolittle’s encounter with the phonetician Henry Higgins. The edition includes the full Preface and What Happened Afterwards, with act summaries, explanatory footnotes and historical context.

Astor annotated edition
Paperback · Edited by Haydn Wood

What this Astor edition contains

  • Complete 1916 Constable text
  • Shaw’s Preface and What Happened Afterwards
  • Act summaries and explanatory footnotes
  • Historical context on language, class and performance

About this edition

This edition presents the play before the additions made for the 1938 film. Act summaries and contextual notes accompany the complete text, including Shaw’s sequel.

The supporting material covers Shaw’s life, London in 1912, phonetics and class, Covent Garden’s flower trade, censorship, the disputed ending and My Fair Lady.
