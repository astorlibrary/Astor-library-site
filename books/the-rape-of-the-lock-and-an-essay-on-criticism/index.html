Alexander Pope

The Rape of the Lock and An Essay on Criticism

The Rape of the Lock turns the cutting of Arabella Fermor’s hair into an elaborate mock-epic of sylphs, fashionable society, ombre and heroic combat. An Essay on Criticism sets out Pope’s arguments about nature, taste, learning, poetic technique and the proper conduct of the critic.

Astor annotated edition

Text and supporting material

  • Both poems complete and unabridged
  • Line numbering throughout
  • Explanatory footnotes keyed to the text
  • Historical context, critical perspectives and glossary

This edition presents the poems in their later forms and adds original material on Pope’s life, sources and influences, eighteenth-century society, the game of ombre, the reading public, reception history and the critical debates surrounding both works. The manuscript’s editorial apparatus is extensive, running from historical context and character studies through critical perspectives, glossary and teaching pathways.
