Arthur Conan Doyle

The Hound of the Baskervilles

When Sir Charles Baskerville dies on the edge of Dartmoor, the evidence points towards an old family legend: a monstrous hound said to pursue the Baskerville line. Sherlock Holmes is sceptical, but the arrival of Sir Henry Baskerville brings new threats and sends Dr Watson to the moor in search of an explanation.

First serialised in The Strand Magazine from August 1901 to April 1902, The Hound of the Baskervilles appeared in book form on 25 March 1902. The first edition contained sixteen of Sidney Paget’s sixty illustrations for the serial.
