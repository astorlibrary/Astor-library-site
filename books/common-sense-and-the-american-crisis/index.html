Thomas Paine

Common Sense and The American Crisis

Common Sense first appeared anonymously in Philadelphia on 10 January 1776. This edition follows the enlarged Bradford text dated 14 February, including the Appendix and Address to the People called Quakers.

Text and supporting material

  • The complete enlarged 1776 text of Common Sense
  • All 16 pieces of The American Crisis
  • Section summaries and explanatory footnotes
  • Historical context, critical perspectives and glossary

The American Crisis follows the war from the retreat of 1776 through independence and peace, comprising thirteen numbered papers, the Crisis Extraordinary and two Supernumerary papers. The edition adds original material on Paine, printing and piracy, political and military context, contemporary rebuttals and later reception.
