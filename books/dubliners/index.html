James Joyce

Dubliners

Joyce’s fifteen stories of Dublin life are presented in the text of the 1914 first edition, with explanatory notes and a summary and context section after every story.

Astor annotated edition
Paperback · Edited by Haydn Wood

View paperback edition

About this edition

This edition includes all fifteen stories, from The Sisters to The Dead. Explanatory footnotes accompany the text, while the supporting material traces the book’s difficult route to publication and its later reception.

The historical material covers Dublin’s streets and institutions, Catholicism, politics, work, emigration, music and language, alongside the stories’ adaptations.
