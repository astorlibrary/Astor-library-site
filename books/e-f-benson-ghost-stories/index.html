E. F. Benson Ghost Stories

Combining The Room in the Tower and Other Stories (1912) and Visible and Invisible (1923), this volume brings together two of E. F. Benson’s major supernatural collections in full. The two books contain seventeen and twelve stories respectively.

Astor annotated edition
Paperback · Edited by Haydn Wood
