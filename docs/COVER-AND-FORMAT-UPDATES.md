# Cover and format conventions

Use the cover filenames supplied in GitHub as the format instruction:

- `Hardcover`, `Hardback` and the spelling `harcover` identify hardback artwork.
- `Main` identifies paperback artwork. Do not put a hardback cover in the primary paperback catalogue or on its paperback buying row.
- `Introduced` identifies the separate introduced edition. It does not replace the main/commentary paperback cover.
- Preserve original asset names, including harmless spelling and capitalisation differences. Match title and format together.

On 25 October 2026, the introduced Frankenstein buying row was corrected to `Frankenstein Introduced.png`. Its commentary paperback remains `IMG_5297.png`; the paired hardback panel now uses that commentary cover too. The introduced Moby-Dick row already correctly uses `Moby Dick Introduced.jpeg` and the commentary paperback uses `Moby Dick.png`. The existing separate introduction covers for Dracula and Great Expectations remain in place; no newly named `Introduced` asset for either was present in the verified GitHub snapshot.

## Hardbacks withdrawn from the current catalogue

The hardbacks of The War of the Worlds, The Chimes, The Haunted Man and the Ghost’s Bargain, The Cricket on the Hearth and The Call of the Wild are no longer listed in the hardback collection or their books’ format choices. Their paperback/Kindle editions remain where already listed. The five original cover files are preserved as source assets. Their established `/go/...-hardback` routes remain registered for existing bookmarks, but the current website no longer advertises those routes.

The format generator removes an old paired paperback/hardback panel whenever that book is absent from the active hardback registry. Deleting a registry record is therefore sufficient to retire its panel on the next library rebuild.
