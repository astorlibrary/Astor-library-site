Much Ado About Nothing · William Shakespeare

Context

Dates

1516
Ariosto’s Orlando Furioso first published Its story of Ginevra, falsely accused after a maid is seen at her window in her mistress’s clothes, is the closest analogue to the Hero plot.
1554
Bandello’s Novelle Another version of the slandered bride, set in Messina, which brings the story to the city Shakespeare uses.
1591
Sir John Harington translates Orlando Furioso The English verse translation makes the Ginevra episode available to readers and playwrights in London.
1598
The play is most likely written Composition is usually placed in 1598 or 1599, at the end of the decade of comedies.
1600
First Quarto Printed by Valentine Simmes for Andrew Wise and William Aspley; its title page links the play to the Lord Chamberlain’s Men.
1613
Performed at court Recorded among the plays given during the celebrations for the marriage of Princess Elizabeth.
1623
First Folio Printed among the comedies, with some of the quarto’s confusing speech prefixes regularised.
1950
Peggy Ashcroft and John Gielgud at Stratford The pairing that began the modern tradition of the play as a vehicle for two major actors.
1976
Judi Dench and Donald Sinden for the RSC One of the best remembered of the company’s many Beatrice and Benedick pairings.
1993
Kenneth Branagh’s film Shot at the Villa Vignamaggio in Tuscany, with Emma Thompson as Beatrice; one of the most commercially successful Shakespeare films.
2012
Iqbal Khan sets the play in contemporary Delhi An RSC production with Meera Syal as Beatrice, staged as part of the World Shakespeare Festival.
2019
Kenny Leon’s Public Theater production is filmed An all-Black cast in a contemporary American political setting, recorded for broadcast.

Places

Messina
The whole play: Leonato’s house, its orchard and garden, a church and a prison.
Padua
Benedick’s city, named in the first scene along with the reputation that precedes him.
Florence
Claudio’s city; the first scene introduces him as a young Florentine who has fought beyond the promise of his age.
Zaragoza
Chief city of Aragon, the kingdom Don Pedro is prince of and the source of the company’s rank.
London
Where the 1600 quarto was printed and where the Lord Chamberlain’s Men first played it.
