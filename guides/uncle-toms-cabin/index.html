Harriet Beecher Stowe

Uncle Tom’s Cabin

A study guide

A Kentucky farmer in debt sells two people to settle it, and the novel follows both of them in opposite directions. Eliza runs north with her child across the ice of the Ohio; Tom is carried south through three owners until he reaches a plantation where the arithmetic of cotton makes a man cheaper to replace than to keep. Stowe built the book out of scenes designed to be unbearable to a churchgoing readership, and then told that readership what it was to do about them.

Written 1851–52; first published 1852.
