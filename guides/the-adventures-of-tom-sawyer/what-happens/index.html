The Adventures of Tom Sawyer · Mark Twain

What happens

Chapters 1–7 The fence, the Sunday school and Becky Thatcher

Tom dodges Aunt Polly, converts a punishment into a profitable enterprise, and exchanges traded tickets for a Bible prize he cannot answer a single question about. Becky Thatcher arrives, is engaged to him inside a day, and discovers she is not the first.

Chapters 8–11 The graveyard, the oath and Muff Potter

Tom and Huck take a dead cat to a new grave to cure warts and see a doctor killed, a drunk framed and a knife left in the right hands. They swear a blood oath to keep mum, and for three chapters the comedy is interrupted by a boy who cannot sleep.

Chapters 12–18 Jackson’s Island and the funeral

Three boys run away to be pirates, live on stolen bacon, corn pone and the fish they catch, and hear their own town firing a cannon over the water to raise their bodies. Tom’s great secret is that they will come back for their own funeral, and the congregation sings the doxology over three boys standing in the aisle.

Chapters 19–24 The trial

Tom takes Becky’s whipping, sits through an examination evening of borrowed compositions, and finally breaks the oath in open court. Muff Potter is saved, Injun Joe goes through the window, and Tom becomes a hero by day and a terrified child at night.

Chapters 25–29 Treasure, the haunted house and the revenge job

A game of digging for buried money turns real when two men count out gold under the floor of a haunted house. Huck follows them, learns that the Widow Douglas is the object of a revenge, and fetches the Welshman and his sons up Cardiff Hill in the middle of the night.

Chapters 30–33 McDougal’s cave, and Injun Joe’s death

Tom and Becky are lost in McDougal’s cave for three days, and Tom sees a hand holding a candle that belongs to the man he testified against. The cave door is sealed by the town, and when it is opened again Injun Joe is lying dead against it with a broken knife.

Chapters 34–35 Twelve thousand dollars and a hogshead

The boys are rich, invested at six per cent, and Huck is taken into the Widow Douglas’s house, where he is washed, combed and made to eat by the clock. He lasts three weeks, and Tom can only get him back by promising that the robber gang has an entry requirement: respectability.
