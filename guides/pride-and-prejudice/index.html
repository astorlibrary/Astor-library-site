Jane Austen

Pride and Prejudice

A study guide

A family of five daughters, an estate entailed away from all of them, and a neighbourhood that treats every new arrival as an opportunity. Elizabeth Bennet is the best reader of character in the book and gets the two most important readings wrong, and Austen builds the plot so that correcting them costs her something. The comedy never lets go of the arithmetic underneath it: who has money, who will inherit, and who can afford to refuse.

Written Drafted 1796–97 as First Impressions; revised 1811–12; first published 1813.
