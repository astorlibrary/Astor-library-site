Study guides/Paradise Lost

John Milton

Paradise Lost

A study guide

A blind republican, on the losing side of a revolution, writes an epic about the first defeat. Milton gives the rebel angel the best speeches and then spends twelve books showing what those speeches lead to, and the crisis of the poem is a conversation between a husband and a wife about whether to work apart for a morning. It ends with two people walking into an unmapped world, holding hands.

Written Composed largely in the 1650s and early 1660s, by dictation; first printed 1667.
