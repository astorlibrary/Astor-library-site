Edith Wharton

Ethan Frome

A study guide

An engineer stranded by a strike in a New England village becomes curious about a lame, silent man at the post office, and assembles the reason from what the neighbours will tell him. What he arrives at is a winter twenty-four years earlier, a farm that cannot be sold, a wife with symptoms and a young cousin with nowhere else to go. The cruelty of the book lies in what the accident at the end fails to end.

Written Out of an earlier French exercise, Hiver, written while Wharton was living in Paris; first published 1911.
