Lewis Carroll

Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland

A study guide

A child follows a rabbit underground and spends an afternoon among creatures who each treat a private rule as though it were the law. Alice grows, shrinks, is asked who she is by a caterpillar, and is sentenced before she is tried. The comedy is exact: almost every joke depends on someone taking a word at face value, and the book ends when Alice stops going along with the pretence that the court is a court.

Written 1862–64; first published 1865.
