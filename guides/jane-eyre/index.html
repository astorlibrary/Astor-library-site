Charlotte Brontë

Jane Eyre

A study guide

An orphan with no money, no family willing to own her and no beauty to trade on argues her way to the terms she will accept. The novel is subtitled an autobiography and reads like one: it is told entirely from inside Jane’s judgement, Jane speaks to the reader directly at the decisive moments, and she turns down both men who want her until the arrangement is one she can accept without losing herself.

Written 1846–47; first published 1847.
