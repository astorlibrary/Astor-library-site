Fyodor Dostoevsky

White Nights

A study guide

A lonely young man who has lived in Petersburg for eight years without making a friend walks home one night along a canal and finds a girl crying at the railing. Over four nights he tells Nastenka what a dreamer is, she tells him about the lodger who promised to come back for her in a year, and he delivers her letter and falls in love with her. On the fourth night she agrees to marry him, and a minute later the lodger walks past. Dostoevsky called it a sentimental novel. It is a love story, and a close portrait of a man who has lived almost entirely on daydreams.

Written 1848; first published 1848.
