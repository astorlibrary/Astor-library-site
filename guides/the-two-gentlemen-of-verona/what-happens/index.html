The Two Gentlemen of Verona · William Shakespeare

What happens

Act I Leaving home

Valentine sets off from Verona for the Emperor’s court at Milan, teasing Proteus for staying at home for love of Julia. Julia, alone with her waiting-woman Lucetta, pretends to be offended by Proteus’s letter, tears it up, and then kisses the pieces that carry his name. Proteus’s father Antonio, told that his son should see the world, orders him to follow Valentine the next day. Proteus, caught reading Julia’s reply, pretends it is from Valentine, and so gives his father the excuse for the parting he dreads.

1.1
Valentine leaves for Milan, Proteus stays, and Speed trades sheep jokes with Proteus over a letter.
1.2
Julia reviews her suitors with Lucetta, tears up Proteus’s letter, and kisses the pieces.
1.3
Antonio orders his son to Milan, and a lie about a letter hurries the parting on.

Act II Milan

In Milan, Valentine is in love with the Duke’s daughter Silvia, who has him write a love letter to a nameless friend and then gives it back to him. In Verona, Proteus and Julia exchange rings and part, and Lance weeps over his family’s farewell while his dog Crab does not. Proteus arrives in Milan, meets Silvia, and hears from Valentine that they are betrothed and plan to elope by a ladder of cords. Alone, Proteus decides to abandon Julia and betray his friend to the Duke. In Verona, Julia decides to follow Proteus dressed as a page.

2.1
Speed lists the signs of love and sees a joke his master misses.
2.2–2.3
Rings and a silent exit for the lovers, then a weeping servant with a dry-eyed dog.
2.4
Valentine praises his friend to the Duke, and Proteus forgets Julia before the scene is out.
2.6–2.7
Proteus resolves to betray Valentine, and Julia resolves to follow Proteus.

Act III Banishment

Proteus tells the Duke about the elopement. The Duke, pretending he needs advice on wooing a lady of Verona, gets Valentine to recommend a ladder of cords and to lend him his cloak, under which he finds the ladder and a letter to Silvia. Valentine is banished on pain of death, and Proteus, feigning friendship, sees him to the city gate. Lance and Speed read through the list of virtues and vices of the milkmaid Lance loves. The Duke and Thurio then enlist Proteus to slander Valentine to Silvia, and Proteus advises Thurio to serenade her.

3.1
The Duke’s invented lady, Valentine’s advice on ladders, and the letter under the cloak.
3.1
Valentine’s lament that to be banished from Silvia is death, and Proteus’s false comfort.
3.1
Lance’s list of his sweetheart’s qualities, read out by Speed.
3.2
Proteus agrees to speak against his friend and teaches Thurio how to woo.

Act IV Disguises

In the forest, outlaws capture Valentine and, pleased by his looks and his languages, make him their captain. In Milan, Julia, disguised as the page Sebastian, hears Proteus serenade Silvia and hears Silvia reject him. Silvia asks Sir Eglamour to escort her to Valentine. Lance explains how he took a whipping for Crab’s misbehaviour. Proteus hires Sebastian and sends the page to Silvia with the ring Julia gave him. Silvia refuses the ring for Julia’s sake and gives the page her portrait, which Julia compares with her own face.

4.1
Valentine claims a killing to impress the outlaws and becomes their captain.
4.2
Proteus’s serenade, Julia listening in disguise, and Silvia’s refusal from her window.
4.3
Silvia plans her escape with a gentleman she trusts.
4.4
Lance and Crab, Julia hired by the man she loves, and two women who meet over a ring.

Act V The forest

Silvia escapes with Eglamour, and the Duke, Thurio, Proteus and Julia follow. Outlaws capture Silvia and Eglamour runs away. Proteus rescues her, and when she still refuses him he tries to force her. Valentine, who has been watching, steps in and denounces him. Proteus repents, and Valentine forgives him and offers him all that was his in Silvia. Julia faints, hands Proteus the wrong ring and reveals herself, and Proteus returns to her. The outlaws bring in the Duke and Thurio. Thurio gives up Silvia, the Duke accepts Valentine and pardons the outlaws, and the two couples are to marry on the same day.

5.1–5.3
Silvia escapes at evening, the court follows, and the outlaws take her.
5.4
Valentine alone in the forest, Proteus’s assault, and the private wound.
5.4
Forgiveness, an offer, a fainting page, and Julia revealed.
5.4
Thurio yields, the Duke relents, and the outlaws are forgiven.
