Kate Chopin

The Awakening

A study guide

A New Orleans broker’s wife spends a summer at a Gulf resort, learns to swim at twenty-eight, and comes back to the city unwilling to keep her Tuesdays at home. Chopin tracks what follows with almost no editorial comment: the painting, the money, the smaller house, the two men, the friend who tells her to think of the children. The novel was reviewed as immoral, fell out of print, and was read properly seventy years later.

Written 1897–98; first published 1899.
