Wilkie Collins

The Moonstone

A study guide

A diamond taken during the storming of Seringapatam arrives in a Yorkshire drawing room as a birthday present and vanishes the same night. Collins gives the case to eleven witnesses in turn, each writing to order and each certain of a different thing, so the mystery is solved less by a detective than by comparing accounts that do not fit. The solution turns on a dose of laudanum given without the patient’s knowledge, and the stone goes back to India.

Written 1867–68; first published 1868.
