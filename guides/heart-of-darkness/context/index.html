Heart of Darkness · Joseph Conrad

Context

Dates

1857
Józef Teodor Konrad Korzeniowski is born On 3 December in Berdychiv, then in the Russian Empire, to a Polish family; his father was imprisoned for nationalist activity, and he was orphaned at eleven.
1874
To sea from Marseilles Conrad joins French merchant ships; in 1878 he moves to British ships, where he serves for sixteen years.
1885
The Congo Free State The great powers recognise a vast territory in central Africa as the personal possession of King Leopold II of the Belgians.
1886
British subject Conrad is naturalised as a British subject.
1890
Conrad in the Congo He keeps a diary from Matadi in June, walks overland to Stanley Pool, and goes up the river as second in command of the steamer Roi des Belges towards Stanley Falls.
1895
Almayer’s Folly Conrad’s first novel, set in Borneo.
1898
Youth Marlow makes his first appearance, in a story Conrad wrote for Blackwood’s Magazine.
1899
The Heart of Darkness in Blackwood’s Three monthly parts, February to April, beginning in the magazine’s thousandth number.
1902
Youth: A Narrative, and Two Other Stories Heart of Darkness appears in book form on 13 November, between Youth and The End of the Tether.
1904
The Casement Report The British consul Roger Casement’s report on abuses in the rubber regions of the Congo Free State is published, and the Congo Reform Association is founded.
1908
Belgium annexes the Congo The Congo Free State ends and becomes the Belgian Congo.
1917
The Author’s Note For a new edition Conrad describes the story as experience pushed a little beyond the actual facts, to bring it home to the reader.
1924
Conrad dies On 3 August, at his home in Kent.
1925
The Hollow Men T. S. Eliot takes the epigraph of his poem from the book: Mistah Kurtz, he dead.
1975
Achebe’s lecture Chinua Achebe’s lecture at the University of Massachusetts, published in 1977 as An Image of Africa, attacks the book’s picture of Africans and changes how it is taught.
1979
Apocalypse Now Francis Ford Coppola moves the story to the Vietnam War.

Places

The Thames estuary, off Gravesend
Where the Nellie waits for the tide, and where Marlow tells the story.
Brussels
Where Conrad signed on in 1890; in the story an unnamed city like a whited sepulchre.
Matadi
The company station below the rapids, where Conrad’s Congo diary begins in June 1890; the model for Marlow’s first station.
Stanley Pool (Kinshasa)
The end of Conrad’s overland march from Matadi in 1890, where he joined the Roi des Belges; the model for the Central Station.
Stanley Falls (Kisangani)
The last navigable reach of the upper river, which the Roi des Belges reached in 1890; the model for the Inner Station.
Berdychiv
Where Conrad was born in 1857, then in the Russian Empire, now in Ukraine.
