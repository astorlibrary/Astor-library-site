William Shakespeare

Julius Caesar

A study guide

A man who has beaten everyone else is offered a crown three times and refuses it three times, and a group of senators kill him for what he might become. The killing happens in the third act, which leaves two acts for the discovery that removing a ruler settles nothing: the crowd changes sides at a funeral, the conspirators quarrel about money, and the republic they killed for ends at Philippi.

Written 1599; first printed 1623.
