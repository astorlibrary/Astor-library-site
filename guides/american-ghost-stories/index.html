F. Marion Crawford, Ambrose Bierce, Ellen Glasgow and others

American Ghost Stories

A study guide

Ten American ghost stories from the half-century when the magazines ran on them. Crawford puts something cold and wet in the upper berth of a transatlantic liner; Bierce introduces his haunted house in the voice of the local paper; Glasgow’s nurse and Freeman’s three sisters see what the men of the house would rather they didn’t; and Olivia Howard Dunbar tells her story from the other side, as a dead wife watching her husband and her sister fall in love. Seven of the ten writers are women, and the edition’s essays follow how the stories were bought, edited and kept in print.

Written Between 1878 and 1925, mostly for magazines and annuals, by ten writers of whom seven are women; first published 1878.
