Study guides/The Willows

Algernon Blackwood

The Willows

A study guide

Two men canoeing down the Danube camp on a sandy island in a wilderness of willows, and the island begins to shrink. A paddle goes missing, the canoe is found slit, funnel-shaped hollows appear in the sand, and a humming like a gong comes from a direction neither of them can name. The Swede works out what is happening and tells his companion the worst part of it: whatever is pressing through here has no interest in human beings at all, and the only way to survive is to keep it out of your mind. Nothing is ever shown. It is the most frightening story of its kind in English.

Written Out of a canoe journey Blackwood made down the Danube in the summer of 1900; first published 1907.
