Wilkie Collins

The Woman in White

A study guide

A drawing master meets a frightened woman on the Finchley Road at midnight and learns afterwards that she has escaped from an asylum. Her resemblance to the heiress he is about to teach turns out to be the whole machinery of a conspiracy: two women can be exchanged if the documents say so, and a marriage settlement supplies the motive. Collins tells it as a file of statements by different hands, so that the reader assembles the case as a jury would, out of testimony that nobody has cross-examined.

Written Begun 15 August 1859; the manuscript finished 26 July 1860, with the serial still running; first published 1860.
