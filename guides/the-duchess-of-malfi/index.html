John Webster

The Duchess of Malfi

A study guide

A young widowed duchess, told by her two brothers never to marry again, proposes to her steward and marries him in her own chamber with her maid hidden behind the arras as witness. The brothers have already planted a spy in her household. The secret holds for years and three children, until a dropped horoscope and a false key bring Ferdinand to her bedside. What follows is imprisonment staged as theatre, a strangling, and a revenge by the spy who did the work, in which every blow lands on the wrong person or too late.

Written c. 1612–14; first printed 1623.
