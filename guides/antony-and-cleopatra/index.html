Study guides/Antony and Cleopatra

William Shakespeare

Antony and Cleopatra

A study guide

A triumvir who governs a third of the known world will not leave Alexandria, and Rome sends messengers instead of armies. Shakespeare gives the story forty-two scenes and no settled vantage point: every judgement passed on Antony is passed by someone with an interest in it. After Actium the play stops being about who wins and becomes about who gets to describe the losers, and Cleopatra spends the last act taking that job away from Caesar.

Written c. 1606–07; first printed 1623.
