Study guides/Carmilla

Sheridan Le Fanu

Carmilla

A study guide

Laura, nineteen, lives with her English father and two governesses in an isolated castle in Styria. One moonlit evening a carriage overturns at their gate, and a lady in black velvet, pleading a journey of life and death, leaves her lovely, languid daughter in their care. Carmilla becomes Laura’s passionate friend. She comes down at one in the afternoon, avoids prayers, will not say who she is, and looks exactly like a portrait dated 1698. Young women in the district begin to die, and Laura sickens. Then General Spielsdorf arrives with the story of how his ward died, and in a ruined chapel the three of them meet her. Laura tells the story years later, to a correspondent, and Le Fanu presents it as one of the papers of a German physician, Doctor Hesselius.

Written 1871; first published 1871.
