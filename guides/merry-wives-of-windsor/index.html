William Shakespeare

The Merry Wives of Windsor

A study guide

Falstaff, short of money at the Garter Inn in Windsor, sends the same love letter to two married women, Mistress Ford and Mistress Page, hoping to get at their husbands’ money. The wives compare letters and decide to punish him. Three times they draw him on: he is carried out in a basket of dirty laundry and tipped into the Thames, beaten out of the house dressed as an old woman, and finally lured to Herne’s oak in Windsor Park at midnight wearing antlers, where children dressed as fairies pinch and burn him. Ford, whose jealousy the wives also cure, has meanwhile disguised himself as ‘Master Brook’ and paid Falstaff to seduce his own wife. Beside this runs the contest for the Pages’ daughter Anne: her father wants Slender, her mother wants Doctor Caius, and Anne marries Fenton.

Written c. 1597–1601; first printed 1602.
