Study guides/The Call of the Wild

Jack London

The Call of the Wild

A study guide

A gardener’s helper with gambling debts sells a judge’s dog to the Klondike trade, and Buck is carried north into a labour market that values him by the pound. London writes the change that follows as an education: the club teaches law, the traces teach work, and every step towards the wolves is paid for in hunger, frostbite and the deaths of other dogs. The book is told from inside an animal that cannot explain itself, so its conclusions have to be shown in behaviour.

Written 1902, after London’s winter in the Yukon; first published 1903.
