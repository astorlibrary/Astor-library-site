The Call of the Wild · Jack London

Context

Dates

1876
Jack London born in San Francisco Born John Griffith Chaney; he took the surname of his stepfather, John London.
1896
Gold found on a tributary of the Klondike The strike that emptied the Pacific ports of strong dogs the following summer.
1897
London travels north to the Klondike He went in over the Chilkoot route in the first season of the stampede and wintered in the Yukon.
1898
London returns to California He came back with almost no gold and with the material for the Northland stories that made his name.
1903
The Call of the Wild serialised and published Five instalments in The Saturday Evening Post, then the Macmillan book, in a green pictorial binding with colour plates.
1903
The argument about animal minds reaches the magazines Naturalists began attacking popular animal stories for granting their subjects human reasoning, a charge London later answered directly.
1906
White Fang published London called it a companion piece, reversing the journey: a wild dog brought into domestication.
1916
London dies at Glen Ellen, California He was forty, and one of the first American writers to have made a large income from fiction alone.
1923
First feature film adaptation Produced by Hal Roach and directed by Fred Jackman, beginning a century of screen versions.
1935
William A. Wellman’s film with Clark Gable The studio version enlarges Thornton and adds a romance, a pattern most later adaptations follow.
2020
Chris Sanders’s film with a digital Buck The animal is computer-generated throughout, which changes what a performance by Buck can be.

Places

Santa Clara Valley, California
Judge Miller’s place, with its cement swimming tank and its Mexican hairless: the sun-kissed Southland Buck is stolen from.
Seattle
Where the crate is opened with a hatchet and the man in the red sweater sells Buck on for three hundred dollars of government money.
Dyea, Alaska
The beach where Curly is killed within hours of landing; the town itself was abandoned within a few years of the rush.
Chilkoot Pass
The climb the team makes on its first day out, and the reason strong dogs were worth stealing in California.
Lake Laberge
Lake Le Barge in the novel, where Buck first fights Spitz over a nest dug in the snow.
Dawson City
The end of the mail run, the saloon where the thousand-pound wager is made, and the last town before the unmapped East.
