The Call of the Wild · Jack London

What happens

Chapter 1 Into the Primitive

Buck is stolen from Judge Miller’s estate, crated, starved, and beaten into submission by a man with a club. In this chapter a house dog becomes freight, and the beating is presented as a lesson: he learns that a man with a club is to be obeyed, and never forgets it.

Chapter 2 The Law of Club and Fang

Curly is killed in two minutes on the Dyea beach, and Buck understands the rule of the place before anyone states it. He learns to sleep under the snow, to steal food without being caught and to pull in harness, and London is careful to record each of these as a loss as well as a skill.

Chapter 3 The Dominant Primordial Beast

The rivalry with Spitz turns into open war over a nest in the snow, and Buck begins undermining the lead dog’s authority by protecting the shirkers. The rabbit hunt ends in the fight that kills Spitz, and Buck wins it by cunning more than weight.

Chapter 4 Who Has Won to Mastership

Buck takes the lead by accepting no other place in the team, and drives it harder than Spitz ever did. The record run to Skagway is followed by the death of Dave, who cannot bear to be taken out of the traces, and by the sale of the whole team to men who know nothing.

Chapter 5 The Toil of Trace and Trail

Hal, Charles and Mercedes load too much, feed too much, then feed nothing, and beat dogs that can no longer stand. The chapter is the book’s clearest statement that cruelty in the North is usually incompetence, and it ends with the ice giving way beneath the sled Buck has been cut out of.

Chapter 6 For the Love of a Man

John Thornton nurses Buck back to strength and receives the only love Buck has ever given. The thousand-pound wager at Dawson makes that love a public spectacle, and Buck’s rescues of Thornton show devotion acting faster than obedience.

Chapter 7 The Sounding of the Call

In the unmapped East, Buck hunts a bull moose for days and runs with a timber wolf, returning to camp each time because Thornton is there. The Yeehats kill Thornton, Buck kills the Yeehats, and the last tie to man is cut by other men.
