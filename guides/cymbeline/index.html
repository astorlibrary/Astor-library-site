William Shakespeare

Cymbeline

A study guide

Cymbeline’s daughter Imogen has married a poor gentleman, Posthumus, and her father has banished him. In Rome a stranger named Iachimo bets that he can seduce her, fails, hides in a trunk in her bedroom, and comes away with enough true detail to pass as proof. What follows is a princess walking across Britain in boy’s clothes, two lost princes keeping house in a Welsh cave, a Roman invasion, and a last scene that unties every knot with the whole cast on stage.

Written c. 1610; first printed 1623.
