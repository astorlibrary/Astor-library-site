Study guides

Free guides to 109 books in the Astor catalogue. Each one covers what happens, the characters, the themes, key quotations, form and language, and the book’s historical context. Every quotation has been checked against a named text of the book.

The guides are free to read and need no account. They are separate from the Astor Study Editions, which are printed books.
