Study guides

Guides to 109 books in the Astor catalogue, free to read here with no account. Each one covers what happens, the characters, the themes, key quotations, form and language, and the book’s historical context. Every quotation has been checked against a named text of the book.

Two other things on the site have “guide” or “study” in their names. The Study Editions are printed books. The slide guides are illustrated slides, which ask for a free account after their first three.
