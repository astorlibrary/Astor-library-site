Mary Shelley

Frankenstein

A study guide

A student in Ingolstadt works out how to animate dead matter, succeeds, looks at what he has made and runs out of the room. The being he abandons teaches itself to speak and read, is driven off by everyone who sees it, and comes back to demand a companion. Shelley builds the whole argument as a set of nested testimonies, so that neither maker nor made ever gets to tell the story alone.

Written Begun in the summer of 1816; first published 1818.
