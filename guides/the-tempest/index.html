William Shakespeare

The Tempest

A study guide

A magician who was once Duke of Milan raises a storm to bring his enemies ashore on the island where he has lived in exile for twelve years. He has the whole afternoon planned before the play begins, and the interest lies in watching a man with absolute power discover what he actually wants to do with it. Almost alone among Shakespeare’s plays it stays in a single place over a few hours, so the action and the performance take roughly the same time.

Written c. 1610–11; first printed 1623.
