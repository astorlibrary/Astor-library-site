Herman Melville

Moby-Dick

A study guide

A schoolmaster with no money and a damp November in his soul signs on to a Nantucket whaler, and finds that the captain means to spend the voyage hunting the white whale that took his leg. Melville keeps interrupting the chase with sermons, stage directions, dictionary entries and a whole natural history of the animal, so that the book takes the shape of Ishmael’s wandering mind, while Ahab’s runs on one track. Everyone aboard dies except the man telling the story, and he survives on a coffin.

Written 1850–51; first published 1851.
