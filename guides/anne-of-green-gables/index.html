L. M. Montgomery

Anne of Green Gables

A study guide

Two elderly unmarried siblings send to an orphan asylum for a boy to help on the farm and are sent a talkative eleven-year-old girl instead. What follows is not a story about a child charming everybody, but about the slow and often reluctant conversion of a household and a village: Marilla learns to say what she feels only when it is nearly too late, and Anne learns which of her gifts are worth keeping. Montgomery puts the comedy of a broken slate and a green-dyed head beside a bereavement and a narrowed future, and lets the second give the first its weight.

Written Begun at Cavendish in June 1905; first published 1908.
