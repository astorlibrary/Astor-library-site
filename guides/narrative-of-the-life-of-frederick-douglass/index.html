Frederick Douglass

Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass

A study guide

Douglass opens by saying what he does not know: his age, his birthday, his father. From that missing record he builds an argument, because the record is missing by design. The book follows a boy from a Maryland plantation to a Baltimore kitchen where a woman begins to teach him the alphabet, to a field where he fights the man hired to break him, and finally to a Massachusetts wharf where he is paid for his labour. It is testimony, and it was published while its author could still be seized and sent back.

Written 1844–45; first published 1845.
