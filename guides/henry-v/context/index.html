Henry V · William Shakespeare

Context

Dates

1413
Henry V succeeds his father Henry IV had taken the crown from Richard II in 1399, which is the fault the king prays about before Agincourt.
1415
The Battle of Agincourt, 25 October Fought on Saint Crispin’s Day after the siege of Harfleur and a long, sick march towards Calais.
1420
The Treaty of Troyes Henry was recognised as heir to the French crown and married Katharine of Valois.
1422
Henry dies; his infant son succeeds Henry VI was not yet a year old, which is the fact the Epilogue closes on.
1587
Holinshed’s Chronicles reissued Shakespeare’s principal source for the campaign, alongside Edward Hall.
1598
The Famous Victories of Henry the Fifth printed An anonymous earlier play covering the same career, which had already made the wild prince and the tennis balls familiar.
1599
Henry V written and first performed The fifth Chorus’s general returning from Ireland is almost certainly the Earl of Essex, who left in March and came home disgraced in September.
1600
The first quarto About half the length of the Folio text, with no Chorus speeches and far less of the night-time debate among the soldiers.
1623
First Folio The fuller text, and the basis of modern editions.
1944
Laurence Olivier’s film Made during the Second World War, and shaped throughout by that purpose: the Harfleur threat and the order to kill the prisoners are not in it.
1989
Kenneth Branagh’s film A muddier, more exhausted Agincourt, which restored much of what the 1944 version had removed.

Places

London
The council chamber of Act I, the Eastcheap of Act II, and the city that welcomes the king home in the fifth Prologue.
Southampton
Where the fleet gathers and the three conspirators are exposed and executed.
Harfleur
The town besieged in Act III, and the gates at which Henry describes what a sack would look like.
Azincourt
The village in Picardy that gave its name to the battle of 25 October 1415.
Calais
The English-held port the exhausted army was marching towards when the French cut them off.
Troyes
Where the treaty of Act V was made and the marriage agreed.
Monmouth
Henry’s birthplace, and the town Fluellen compares to Macedon on the grounds that both have a river.
