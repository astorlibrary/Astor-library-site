Carnacki, the Ghost-Finder · William Hope Hodgson

Themes

Method

Before anything supernatural is entertained, Carnacki eliminates people: tape across the doors, wax on the windows, photographs of every wall. The haunting only begins once fraud has been ruled out in front of the reader.

The method is set out in the first case, tested in the middle ones, and in the last it catches a hoax instead of a ghost, which proves the method was never rigged.

Occult learning

The stories assume a profession: manuscripts to consult, monographs to read, rituals with numbered signs, and defences that can be built wrong. Carnacki’s authority comes from reading; he claims no gift.

Each case adds a scrap of the system (the Saaamaaa Ritual, the Unknown Last Line, the Incantation of Raaaee), and none of it is ever explained fully enough to be checked.

The Electric Pentacle

The Electric Pentacle is the book’s best idea: the mediaeval figure of protection, wired with vacuum tubes and run off accumulators. Edwardian electricity meets a fourteenth-century manuscript and neither is treated as absurd.

It is introduced as an improvement, relied on in every serious case, and its weakness (it can be broken by a hand reaching across it) makes the first story frightening.

The storytelling evening

The card, the dinner, the refusal to be hurried, the story told without interruption, the dismissal. The frame is as fixed as the pentacle, and it does the same job: it holds a shape around something unruly.

Six stories, one unvarying evening, and the reader comes to want the closing words as much as the guests do.

A protective power

The one piece of comfort in Hodgson’s universe is stated in an aside: an inscrutable protective force intervenes between the human soul and what is outside. It is not called God and it does not always arrive early.

It saves Carnacki in the third case, is explained to Arkright in the same story, and is never claimed as certain.

Admitting fear

Carnacki reports his own terror as part of the evidence: the sweat, the cowed feeling, the spiritual sickness that comes with the ab-human. In a genre of unflappable experts, that is the collection’s most modern quality.

He is frightened in the first story and still frightened in the sixth, and never pretends the experience gets easier.
