William Hope Hodgson

Carnacki, the Ghost-Finder

A study guide

Carnacki investigates haunted houses the way a surveyor investigates subsidence: he measures the room, photographs it, seals the doors with tape, chalks a circle, and sits inside a ring of vacuum tubes he calls the Electric Pentacle. Some of the six cases turn out to be frauds and he says so; some are not, and the defences barely hold. The pattern never varies: dinner, the story, and then Carnacki’s cheerful ‘Out you go!’ It is the one thing in the book that can be relied on.

Written 1910–12, for The Idler and The New Magazine; first published 1910.
