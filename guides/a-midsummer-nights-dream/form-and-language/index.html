A Midsummer Night's Dream · William Shakespeare

Form and language

Verse registers

Giving different groups of characters distinct metres and forms.

The court speaks blank verse, the lovers slide into rhymed couplets as they become more ridiculous, the fairies use a short trochaic line, and the mechanicals speak prose except when performing, where they are given deliberately terrible verse.

Love looks not with the eyes, but with the mind; And therefore is wing'd Cupid painted blind:

Helena, 1.1.234

Play within a play

A staged performance watched by characters who are themselves being watched.

Pyramus and Thisbe is a tragedy of parental prohibition and mistaken death, performed for four people who have just escaped one. The court’s mockery is aimed at conventions the audience has been accepting all evening.

Lord, what fools these mortals be!

Puck, 3.2.115

Doubling

One actor taking two parts, so that the audience sees a resemblance the text does not state.

Theseus and Oberon, Hippolyta and Titania, and Philostrate and Puck have often been doubled since Peter Brook’s 1970 production, so the fairy quarrel looks like the same marriage seen at night.

Bathos

A sudden drop from the elevated to the ridiculous.

It drives the whole last act (“the wall is down that parted their fathers”) and the wood too, where a fairy queen’s love speech is answered by a weaver talking about the difficulty of getting out of a wood.

Let me play the lion too: I will roar, that I will do any man's heart good to hear me;

Bottom, 1.2.58

The catalogue

A list used to build a world faster than description can.

Oberon’s bank of wild thyme, oxlips, violets, woodbine, musk-roses and eglantine gives the wood a physical existence on a bare stage in six lines, and Titania’s list of rotted corn and drowned fields does the same for the damage.

And thorough this distemperature we see The seasons alter: hoary-headed frosts Fall in the fresh lap of the crimson rose;

Titania, 2.1.106

Epilogue

A direct address to the audience after the story has closed.

Puck offers the audience the same excuse the characters have used on themselves, that they were asleep and this was a dream, so accepting the comedy becomes something the spectators have to do themselves.

If we shadows have offended, Think but this, and all is mended, That you have but slumber'd here While these visions did appear.

Puck, 5.1.404
