Jerome K. Jerome

Three Men in a Boat

A study guide

Three young men who have talked themselves into believing they are overworked take a boat from Kingston and row, tow and argue their way up the Thames to Oxford, with a fox terrier. Very little happens: tents collapse, a tin of pineapple will not open, a comic song goes wrong. The telling is the book. J. keeps leaving the river for anecdotes, local history and passages of fine writing, and is brought back each time by a practical question, a wet shirt or his own vanity. It ends with the three of them running away from the rain to a music hall in Leicester Square and drinking to being out of the boat.

Written 1888–89; first published 1889.
