William Shakespeare

Romeo and Juliet

A study guide

A Chorus gives away the ending in fourteen lines, and the play then spends four days showing how quickly two children can reach it. Shakespeare took a story that had run over months in his sources and pressed it into a single hot week, so that every adult delay, every sealed-off street and every letter that fails to arrive lands on lovers who have no time to spare.

Written c. 1594–1596; first printed 1597.
