William Shakespeare

Henry IV, Part 1

A study guide

A king who took the crown from Richard II cannot get out of England to atone for it, because the men who helped him take it now want paying. His heir is drinking in Eastcheap with a fat knight who thinks honour is a word, while the son of the rebel earls is winning the reputation the prince should have. The play cuts between court, tavern and rebel camp until all three arrive on the same field, and lets Hal settle the account in person.

Written c. 1596–97; first printed 1598.
