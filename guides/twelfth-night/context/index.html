Twelfth Night · William Shakespeare

Context

Dates

1581
Barnabe Riche’s Farewell to Military Profession Contains ‘Of Apolonius and Silla’, the twin-and-disguise tale that is the play’s chief source.
1601
The play is most likely written Dated by its style and by the fact that it was already being performed early in 1602.
1602
Performed at Middle Temple Hall On 2 February, recorded in the diary of the law student John Manningham: the earliest surviving notice of the play.
1623
First Folio Printed among the comedies as Twelfe Night, Or what you will; there is no earlier text.
1912
Harley Granville-Barker’s Savoy production A fast, uncut staging on a simple set that helped end the tradition of heavy Victorian scenery in Shakespeare.
1969
John Barton’s production for the Royal Shakespeare Company An autumnal, melancholy reading that shaped how the play was staged for a generation.
1996
Trevor Nunn’s film A screen version that opens with the shipwreck and gives the Malvolio plot its full weight.
2002
An all-male staging at Shakespeare’s Globe Original-practices costume and casting, with Mark Rylance as Olivia, revived a decade later in the West End and on Broadway.

Places

The Illyrian coast
Illyria was the old name for the eastern Adriatic shore; Shakespeare uses it as a place far enough away to be anything he needs.
Middle Temple Hall, London
Where the play’s earliest recorded performance was given on 2 February 1602.
Southwark, London
Antonio’s inn, the Elephant in the south suburbs, borrows the name of a real establishment on the theatre side of the river.
Ware, Hertfordshire
Sir Toby tells Sir Andrew to write a challenge as big as the Great Bed of Ware, a celebrated inn bed and a standing English joke.
