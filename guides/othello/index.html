William Shakespeare

Othello

A study guide

A general marries a senator’s daughter without asking anyone, defends the marriage before the Venetian state, and wins. Then the war he was sent to fight is cancelled by a storm, and his ensign spends one long scene in a garden converting a handkerchief into evidence. The play is less about jealousy than about what a man will accept as proof when the person offering it has never once been wrong.

Written c. 1603–04; first printed 1622.
