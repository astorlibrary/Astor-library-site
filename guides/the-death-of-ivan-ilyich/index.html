Leo Tolstoy

The Death of Ivan Ilyich

A study guide

The story begins with the news of a judge’s death and his colleagues’ first thought, which is of the promotions it will bring. Then it goes back over his life: a correct, agreeable career in the law, a marriage kept at arm’s length, a new house furnished to look like everyone else’s, and a knock against a window frame while he is hanging curtains. The pain in his side becomes the centre of his existence. Doctors talk of kidneys and appendixes, his family pretend he is not dying, and only the peasant servant Gerasim treats him honestly. In his last days Ivan Ilych asks whether his whole life has been wrong, and two hours before he dies he finds light where he expected death.

Written 1884–1886; first published 1886.
