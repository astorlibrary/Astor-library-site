Study guides/Gulliver’s Travels

Jonathan Swift

Gulliver’s Travels

A study guide

A ship’s surgeon writes up four voyages in the flat, dated, measured prose of factual travel literature, and each one leaves him a worse judge of his own species than the last. He is a giant, then a pet, then a tourist among projectors and immortals, and finally a man who cannot bear the smell of his wife. Swift’s method is to keep the reporting exact and let the reader supply the judgement that Gulliver withholds.

Written In the 1720s; first published 1726.
