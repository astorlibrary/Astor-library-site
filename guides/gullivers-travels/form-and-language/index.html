Gulliver’s Travels · Jonathan Swift

Form and language

Mock travel writing

Borrowing the apparatus of factual voyage literature (dates, bearings, cargoes, vocabularies) for an invented country.

Each voyage opens with a ship, a date and a latitude, and the first edition carried maps; the fiction is guaranteed by exactly the machinery that guaranteed Dampier.

they shouted for joy, and danced upon my breast, repeating several times as they did at first, “Hekinah degul.”

Part I, Chapter I

Deadpan narration

Reporting the outrageous in the tone reserved for the ordinary, leaving the reader to supply the judgement.

Gulliver blames his own want of courtly training for failing to see the lenity of a sentence of blinding, and puts the King of Brobdingnag’s horror at gunpowder down to a nice, unnecessary scruple. Both times the flat voice leaves the reader to supply the judgement.

It is allowed on all hands, that the primitive way of breaking eggs, before we eat them, was upon the larger end

Part I, Chapter IV

Defamiliarisation

Describing a known thing from outside, in terms that do not name it.

A pocket watch becomes an engine that makes a noise like a water-mill and is filed as a possible god; the law becomes a society bred up to prove that white is black, and a lie becomes the thing which was not.

we conjecture it is either some unknown animal, or the god that he worships; but we are more inclined to the latter opinion

Part I, Chapter II

Satirical escalation

Arranging a sequence so that each part is harder to laugh off than the last.

Rope-dancers, then gunpowder, then the Academy, then a reasoned proposal to exterminate a species: the structure is a slope.

I cannot but conclude the bulk of your natives to be the most pernicious race of little odious vermin that nature ever suffered to crawl upon the surface of the earth.”

Part II, Chapter VI

Mock documents

Prefaces, letters and publisher’s notes that stand outside the narrative and vouch for it.

Sympson certifies Gulliver’s truthfulness, and Gulliver then attacks Sympson for corrupting the text, so the guarantee and the complaint cancel each other before Chapter I.

I do, in the next place, complain of my own great want of judgment, in being prevailed upon by the entreaties and false reasoning of you and some others, very much against my own opinion, to suffer my travels to be published.

A Letter from Captain Gulliver to His Cousin Sympson

Exact measurement

Precise numbers used where a vague impression would do.

Six inches, a twelfth, 1,728 Lilliputian meals, the compass of a silver threepence: the arithmetic is kept up with care, and it makes the impossible countries feel surveyed.
