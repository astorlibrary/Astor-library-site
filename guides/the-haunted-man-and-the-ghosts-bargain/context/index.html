The Haunted Man and the Ghost’s Bargain · Charles Dickens

Context

Dates

1812
Dickens born in Portsmouth Born on 7 February at Landport, the son of a clerk in the Navy Pay Office.
1843
Dickens visits the Field Lane Ragged School The school for destitute children near Saffron Hill in London, which he described in letters and later in print.
1843
A Christmas Carol published The first of the five Christmas books.
1844
Ragged School Union founded The organisation that brought London’s ragged schools together, with Lord Ashley as its president.
1846
The Battle of Life published The fourth Christmas book.
1847
No Christmas book this year Dickens set the new story aside while he finished Dombey and Son.
1848
Dickens’s sister Fanny dies Fanny Burnett, his elder sister, died in September, three months before the book appeared.
1848
The Haunted Man published Bradbury and Evans issued it on 19 December, the last of the Christmas books.
1848
18,000 copies sold on the first day The figure recorded by the Charles Dickens Museum.
1862
Pepper’s Ghost John Henry Pepper staged a scene from the story at the Royal Polytechnic Institution with the illusion that took his name.
1870
Dickens dies at Gad’s Hill He died on 9 June.

Places

London
The unnamed old college, squeezed by the city, and Jerusalem Buildings close by.
Devonshire Terrace, London
Dickens’s home when the book was published.
Royal Polytechnic Institution, Regent Street
Where Pepper staged a scene from the story with his ghost illusion.
Field Lane, Saffron Hill
The ragged school Dickens visited in 1843.
