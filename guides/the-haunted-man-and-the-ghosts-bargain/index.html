Charles Dickens

The Haunted Man and the Ghost’s Bargain

A study guide

Redlaw, a famous chemist who lives alone in an old London college, is haunted by the memory of a neglected childhood, a friend who took the woman he loved, and a sister who died. On Christmas Eve a phantom with his own face offers to cancel his memory of sorrow, wrong and trouble, and he accepts. The gift spreads to everyone he meets. Tenderness, gratitude and forgiveness go with the painful memories: a loving family turns sour, an old man disowns his dying son, a grateful student insults his nurse. Only two people are proof against it: a starving boy who has never had a kind memory to lose, and Milly Swidger, the keeper’s wife, whose own grief has made her kind.

Written 1848; first published 1848.
