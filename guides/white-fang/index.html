Study guides/White Fang

Jack London

White Fang

A study guide

A cub that is three-quarters wolf is born in a cave beside a frozen stream, walks into an Indian camp, and spends the rest of the book being made into whatever his owners need: a sled dog for Grey Beaver, a pit fighter for Beauty Smith, and at last a house dog for a mining expert’s family in California. London sets out to prove that character is clay pressed by circumstance, and he is thorough enough to apply the same rule to a camp cook and an escaped convict. The difficulty the novel never settles is that its happy ending is also a surrender, and that the thumb which finally presses the wolf into a fairer shape belongs to a man he calls a god.

Written c. 1904–05, in California, as a deliberate companion to The Call of the Wild; first published 1906.
