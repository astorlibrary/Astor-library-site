William Shakespeare

Macbeth

A study guide

Three witches greet a victorious general with a prophecy that he will be king. Within a day he has murdered the king who trusts him, and the rest of the play watches him kill to keep a crown that gives him nothing. It is Shakespeare’s fastest tragedy, and the speed is the argument: Macbeth is never allowed the time to become a different kind of man.

Written c. 1606; first printed 1623.
