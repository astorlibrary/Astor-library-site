Macbeth · William Shakespeare

Context

Dates

1040
The historical Macbeth becomes king of Alba He ruled for seventeen years, a reign Shakespeare compresses to a few violent months.
1057
The historical Macbeth is killed at Lumphanan Not at Dunsinane, and not by Macduff.
1587
Holinshed’s Chronicles reissued Shakespeare’s chief source for the Scottish material, including the meeting with the three weird sisters.
1597
James VI publishes Daemonologie The future king of England writes a dialogue on witchcraft six years before inheriting the English throne.
1603
James VI of Scotland becomes James I of England A Scottish king in London, claiming descent from Banquo, changes what a Scottish play on a London stage can mean.
1605
The Gunpowder Plot The conspiracy and its trials put the word equivocation into everyday London use.
1606
Macbeth most likely written and first performed The Porter’s equivocator is the strongest internal evidence for the date.
1611
Simon Forman records seeing Macbeth at the Globe The earliest surviving eyewitness account of the play in performance.
1623
First Folio The only early printed text of the play, and a short one.
1847
Verdi’s Macbeth premieres in Florence Revised for Paris in 1865, it becomes a performance tradition of its own.
1936
Orson Welles directs the Federal Theatre Project’s Black-cast Macbeth Staged at the Lafayette Theatre in Harlem and relocated to a Caribbean island.

Places

Inverness
Macbeth’s castle, where Duncan is murdered while a guest.
Forres
Duncan’s court, where Macbeth is thanked and Malcolm is named heir.
Dunsinane Hill
Macbeth’s fortress in the last act, and the hill Birnam wood must reach.
Birnam
The wood the soldiers cut for cover, about twelve miles from Dunsinane.
Fife
Macduff’s household, murdered in 4.2.
Iona
Colmekill, where Duncan’s body is carried for burial among his ancestors.
