M. R. James

M. R. James: Collected Ghost Stories

A study guide

A Cambridge provost wrote these to be read aloud at Christmas, and they work the same way on the page. A scholar finds a manuscript, buys a print, takes a whistle from the ruins of a Templar preceptory, and something that has been waiting comes for him, usually at the edge of sight and usually made of the wrong material: crumpled linen, hair, a tentacle, a mouth. James is precise about libraries, brasses and Latin abbreviations, which is exactly why the other thing lands. The collection gathers all four volumes, the 1931 additions and the last stories, with James’s own writing on how to do it.

Written Between 1893 and 1936, most of them read aloud to friends at Christmas before they were printed; first published 1904.
