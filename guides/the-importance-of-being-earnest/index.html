Oscar Wilde

The Importance of Being Earnest

A study guide

Two young men have each invented a person who does not exist in order to get out of obligations, and both find themselves engaged to women who will only marry a man called Ernest. Wilde builds a farce in which the plot is entirely conventional and the sentences are not: every line of feeling arrives inverted, and the discovery that resolves everything is produced out of a hand-bag left in a railway cloakroom.

Written 1894; first published 1899.
