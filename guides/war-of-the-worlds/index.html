H. G. Wells

The War of the Worlds

A study guide

A cylinder from Mars lands on a Surrey common, and within a week the largest empire on Earth has been reduced to six million people running along the roads out of London. Wells tells it through a narrator who is never in charge of anything: he watches, runs, hides in a ruined scullery and arrives too late to understand what he has seen. The Martians are killed by bacteria, and the book is careful to insist that this is not a victory.

Written Mid-1890s, largely at Woking; first published 1898.
