The House on the Borderland · William Hope Hodgson

Context

Dates

1877
Hodgson born in Essex The second of twelve children of an Anglican clergyman who served eleven parishes in twenty-one years.
1891
Hodgson goes to sea as an apprentice Apprenticed at thirteen after a first attempt to run away; eight years at sea gave him the horror of the ocean that runs through all his work.
1899
Hodgson opens a gymnasium in Blackburn He left the sea, opened a gymnasium in Blackburn and photographed storms; the discipline shows in his fiction’s practicality.
1907
Hodgson’s first novel, The Boats of the Glen Carrig His first novel, published the year this one was written.
1907
Author’s introduction dated 17 December 1907 Hodgson signs it as the man the manuscript was handed to, and says he has left it as he received it.
1908
Published by Chapman & Hall Reviewers admired the siege and were baffled by the cosmic chapters.
1912
Hodgson’s The Night Land published His last and longest novel takes the end-of-the-sun material of this book and builds a whole world on it.
1918
Hodgson killed at Ypres He enlisted at thirty-seven, was invalided out, re-enlisted, and was killed by a shell in April 1918.
1934
Lovecraft reads the novel H. C. Koenig lent him Hodgson’s books in August. Lovecraft added a passage on them to Supernatural Horror in Literature, ranking this one perhaps the best, spoiled only by touches of sentimentality.
1946
Arkham House reprint The House on the Borderland and Other Novels put Hodgson back in print in America and fixed his reputation.

Places

Ardrahan, County Galway
The kind of western Irish country the fictional Kraighten is set in, reached by a road that stops.
The Burren, County Clare
Limestone country full of sinkholes and underground rivers, the geology the pit is drawn from.
Blackburn, Lancashire
Where Hodgson ran his School of Physical Culture and began writing.
Borth, Ceredigion
The Welsh coast where he lived while writing his novels.
Ypres, Belgium
Where he was killed in 1918, ten years after this book appeared.
