William Shakespeare

Coriolanus

A study guide

A city short of grain makes a soldier its hero and then asks him to stand in the marketplace, show his scars and ask for votes. He can take a walled town almost single-handed and cannot say a civil sentence to a citizen, so Rome banishes him and he brings the enemy back to its gates. The play gives the crowd real arguments, gives the hero none worth having, and then hands the ending to his mother.

Written c. 1608; first printed 1623.
