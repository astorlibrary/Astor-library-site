G. K. Chesterton

The Man Who Was Thursday

A study guide

Gabriel Syme, a poet who has joined a secret police force of philosophers, talks his way into the Central Anarchist Council and is elected Thursday. Six men sit at the table with him under a vast president called Sunday, and one by one they turn out to be exactly what Syme is. The hunt then reverses: the conspiracy dissolves, the detectives chase the president across two countries, and the book they are in stops being a thriller. Chesterton subtitled it A Nightmare and spent the rest of his life pointing at the subtitle.

Written 1907, in the year between Heretics and Orthodoxy; first published 1908.
