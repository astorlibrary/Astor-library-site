Charles Dickens

The Chimes

A study guide

Toby Veck, known as Trotty, is an old ticket-porter who waits for errands outside a London church. On the last day of the year he listens to gentlemen with statistics and opinions explain what is wrong with the poor, and by nightfall he has come to agree with them. The church bells then call him up the tower and show him what those opinions do to his daughter Meg over the years that follow. He wakes as the bells ring in the New Year, which is also her wedding day.

Written 1844; first published 1844.
