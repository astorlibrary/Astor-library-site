Arthur Conan Doyle

The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

A study guide

Twelve cases written to a monthly deadline, and the book that taught detective fiction its shape: a client with a story they have already misread, a detective who reads the room instead, and a narrator who withholds. Doyle’s London runs on railway timetables, newspaper advertisements and rented lodgings, and the crimes are mostly about money and respectability; there is little murder. Four of the twelve end without an arrest.

Written 1891–92, one story a month; first published 1892.
