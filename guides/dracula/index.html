Bram Stoker

Dracula

A study guide

A solicitor’s clerk goes to Transylvania to complete a property purchase and finds his client is not leaving the country alone. Stoker tells the whole thing in journals, letters, telegrams, ships’ logs, newspaper cuttings and phonograph cylinders, so the reader assembles the danger months before the characters manage to compare notes. The hunt is finally won by filing: Mina types the papers into order, and the order shows them where he has gone.

Written Researched and drafted through the 1890s; first published 1897.
