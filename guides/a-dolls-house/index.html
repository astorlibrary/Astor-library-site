Henrik Ibsen

A Doll's House

A study guide

Nora Helmer forged her dying father’s signature to borrow money that saved her husband’s life, and has been paying it back in secret for years out of the housekeeping. Over three days at Christmas the loan comes due in another way: the man who holds the bond wants his job back, the letter reaches the letterbox, and Nora waits for her husband to do the thing she has always assumed he would do. He does not, and the play ends with the most consequential exit in modern theatre.

Written 1879; first published 1879.
