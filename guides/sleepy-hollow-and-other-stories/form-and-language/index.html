Sleepy Hollow and Other American Halloween Stories · Washington Irving, Edgar Allan Poe and others

Form and language

Found manuscripts

Presenting a tale as something recorded, inherited or overheard.

Irving attributes Sleepy Hollow to the papers of Diedrich Knickerbocker and ends with a note on a man who told it at a corporation meeting. Bierce uses a coroner’s inquest and a dead man’s diary. The distance is part of the joke and part of the fright.

this sequestered glen has long been known by the name of SLEEPY HOLLOW, and its rustic lads are called the Sleepy Hollow Boys throughout all the neighboring country

The Legend of Sleepy Hollow

Unreliable narrators

A first-person account the reader is meant to read against.

Two of Poe’s three stories open with a denial of madness and close with a confession. Gilman’s narrator is wrong about the paper and right about the marriage, which is the harder version of the same device.

Comedy and horror

Keeping a light, ironic tone over frightening material.

Irving spends pages on pies and dancing before anything appears on the road, and describes an heiress in the same breath as her father’s poultry. The laughter makes the ride home work.

A drowsy, dreamy influence seems to hang over the land, and to pervade the very atmosphere.

The Legend of Sleepy Hollow

American settings

Using real, local geography instead of generic gothic scenery.

The Tappan Zee, Tarry Town, the Dutch burying ground, an Ohio clearing. Most of the stories put their horror somewhere a reader could drive to.

this sequestered glen has long been known by the name of SLEEPY HOLLOW, and its rustic lads are called the Sleepy Hollow Boys throughout all the neighboring country

The Legend of Sleepy Hollow

Symbolic objects

A single physical thing that carries the whole tale.

A pumpkin, a veil, a heart, a cat, a boarded window, a pattern on a wall, a mirror. The collection can almost be indexed by its objects.

was found the hat of the unfortunate Ichabod, and close beside it a shattered pumpkin

The Legend of Sleepy Hollow

Withheld endings

Keeping the decisive piece of information out of the story, or until the final sentence.

Irving gives Ichabod two endings, a farmer’s report and the old wives’ story, and does not choose between them; Bierce’s Boarded Window holds its explanation to the last line; Hawthorne refuses to say whether the forest meeting was a dream.

was found the hat of the unfortunate Ichabod, and close beside it a shattered pumpkin

The Legend of Sleepy Hollow

American Gothic

Gothic machinery adapted to a country without ruins.

Apart from Usher’s ancient house, there are no abbeys here and few curses older than the colony. These writers use recent history instead (a Revolutionary cavalryman, a witch trial, a frontier cabin), which is why the stories still feel close.
