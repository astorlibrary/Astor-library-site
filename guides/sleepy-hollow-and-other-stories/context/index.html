Sleepy Hollow and Other American Halloween Stories · Washington Irving, Edgar Allan Poe and others

Context

Dates

1783
The Revolution ends The Hessian trooper of the legend is a soldier of that war, which was within living memory when Irving wrote.
1820
Irving’s ‘The Legend of Sleepy Hollow’ published Published in The Sketch Book of Geoffrey Crayon, Gent., which made Irving the first American writer to win a large readership in Britain.
1824
Irving’s ‘The Devil and Tom Walker’ published Printed in Tales of a Traveller, moving the same comic-gothic method to New England money.
1835
Hawthorne’s ‘Young Goodman Brown’ published Hawthorne, a descendant of a Salem witch-trial judge, publishes the forest story anonymously in a magazine.
1839
Poe’s ‘The Fall of the House of Usher’ published Poe in Burton’s Gentleman’s Magazine, setting the terms for American gothic interiors.
1843
Poe’s ‘The Tell-Tale Heart’ and ‘The Black Cat’ published The Tell-Tale Heart and The Black Cat appear within months of each other.
1891
Bierce’s ‘The Boarded Window’ published Bierce, a Civil War veteran, publishes the frontier story whose last line does all the work.
1892
Charlotte Perkins Gilman’s ‘The Yellow Wall-Paper’ published Gilman’s story of a rest cure appears in The New England Magazine.
1903
Mary Wilkins Freeman’s ‘The Shadows on the Wall’ published Mary Wilkins Freeman’s New England ghost story, told almost entirely through the household’s conversation.
1926
Lovecraft’s ‘The Outsider’ in Weird Tales Lovecraft in Weird Tales, closing the century the collection covers.

Places

Tarrytown, New York
Irving’s Tarry Town, on the Tappan Zee; Sleepy Hollow lies just north of it.
Sleepy Hollow, New York
The Dutch burying ground and the bridge the Horseman must not cross.
Salem, Massachusetts
The witch-trial town behind Hawthorne’s forest and behind Ichabod’s favourite reading.
Boston, Massachusetts
The New England Magazine, which printed The Yellow Wall-Paper in 1892.
Providence, Rhode Island
Lovecraft’s city, and the source of the antiquarian New England in The Outsider.
