Sleepy Hollow and Other American Halloween Stories

A study guide

Twelve American stories that Halloween adopted afterwards. Irving’s schoolmaster rides home past a Dutch burying ground; Hawthorne’s Goodman Brown walks into the forest and comes back unable to look at his neighbours; Poe supplies a house, a cat and a heart under the floorboards; Bierce, Gilman and Freeman work the same ground colder; and Lovecraft’s Outsider climbs out of a castle to find a mirror. Not one was written for the holiday, and the introduction follows how the holiday got them: through jack-o’-lanterns, print, repetition and trade.

Written Between 1820 and 1926, by seven writers who had no October holiday in mind; first published 1820.
