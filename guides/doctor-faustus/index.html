Study guides/Doctor Faustus

Christopher Marlowe

Doctor Faustus

A study guide

A Wittenberg scholar who has run through logic, medicine, law and divinity signs his soul to Lucifer in exchange for twenty-four years with Mephistopheles at his command. He asks for omnipotence and gets a travelling show: a box on the Pope’s ear, grapes in January, a pair of horns on a heckler. The play’s real action is the repentance that is offered in nearly every scene and never taken, and its last hour is the first time Faustus counts time honestly.

Written c. 1588–93 (the date is disputed); first printed 1604.
