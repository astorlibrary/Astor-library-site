Where the books happen.

Inverness, Thornfield, Transylvania, the Pequod’s track. Every place a book in this catalogue puts you, plotted on one map, with a note on what happens there.

The places come from coordinates held with each book’s record, and the coastlines and borders from Natural Earth, which is in the public domain. Both are served from this site: the map loads no tiles and contacts no third party.
